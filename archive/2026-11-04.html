  1. 1

    Worldsheet functional RG and the $α'$-tower

    Ivano Basile, Francesco Ferrarin, Alessia Platania, Matteo Zatti

    The authors apply the functional RG (Wetterich equation) to the bosonic string worldsheet, deriving beta functions for the background fields in a two-derivative truncation and an all-orders-in-alpha-prime truncation. The latter reproduces Tseytlin's spacetime action and is used to find Schwarzschild-like and Einstein solutions.

    Worldsheet functional RG, effective asymptotic safety in string theory; matches wanted paper.

    A Note On Lattice Definition of Gauge Theories With Chiral Fermions

    Edward Witten

    The paper gives a variant of Craig's lattice regularization of chiral gauge theories, interpolating the lattice gauge field to the continuum and coupling it to continuum chiral fermions via the eta-invariant. It argues this works for any gauge group and anomaly-free representation and is computable in principle but not by Monte Carlo.

    arXiv:2611.05051hep-lathep-phhep-thcross-listrelevance 6quality 9

    Lattice chiral gauge theories; reader wanted this paper.

  3. 3

    Inner Product of Closed Universe from Gravitational Path-integral

    Masamichi Miyaji, Sunil Sake, Kotaro Shinmyo

    The paper derives the inner product and Hilbert space of a closed universe in AdS and dS JT gravity directly from the gravitational path integral, showing the co-invariant and invariant spaces of refined algebraic quantization come from different boundary gauge fixings. It computes the inner product exactly for fixed topology and lapse contour.

    arXiv:2611.03789hep-threlevance 6quality 8

    Gravitational path-integral inner product, BFV-BRST quantization of JT gravity.

  4. 4

    Weyl Rescalings: A First Order Approach

    L. Andrianopoli, J. Zanelli

    Shows that the apparent mismatch between first-order (vielbein) and second-order Weyl rescalings is resolved by the torsion induced by the rescaling. It then constructs Weyl-invariant first-order actions for scalars, spinors and gauge fields.

    arXiv:2611.04978hep-thgr-qcrelevance 9quality 6

    Weyl rescalings in first order gravity, spontaneously broken Weyl symmetry; matches wanted paper.

  5. 5

    A Ghost-Free dS/AdS Correspondence in $\mathcal{N}=1$ Supergravity

    Omer Guleryuz

    Constructs a Z2 reflection of the nilpotent goldstino Kahler metric in N=1 supergravity that maps a ghost-free dS theory to a ghost-free AdS theory with V -> -V pointwise. It derives a spectrum dictionary and the stability window 0 <= m^2 <= 9H^2/4, then applies it to anti-D3 uplifts and inflation models.

    arXiv:2611.03977hep-thgr-qcrelevance 8quality 6

    Ghost-free dS/AdS in N=1 supergravity; matches wanted paper, swampland.

  6. 6

    Gauge-invariant non-universal scalar couplings in teleparallel gravity

    Giovanni Otalora, Rudranil Roy

    The paper builds a gauge-invariant teleparallel theory where a scalar field couples non-universally to the translational gauge potential through a Stueckelberg-completed scalar-sector tetrad. In FLRW cosmology it finds kination and potential-dominated regimes and late-time solutions approaching de Sitter with a subdominant matter-like term.

    arXiv:2611.03740gr-qcastro-ph.COhep-threlevance 7quality 6

    Teleparallel gravity with non-universal scalar couplings, cosmology; modified gravity theory.

  7. 7

    Linear perturbation theory and structure formation in a Horndeski theory of gravity without dark matter

    Lorenzo Gervani, Antonaldo Diaferio, Francesco Pace

    Builds a Horndeski generalization of Refracted Gravity without dark matter, checks the background expansion, and develops linear perturbations and the growth equation. The model reproduces H(z) but predicts structure formation that begins too late (z below about 1), so it fails.

    arXiv:2611.05112astro-ph.COgr-qcrelevance 6quality 7

    Horndeski scalar-tensor theory without dark matter, perturbations.

  8. 8

    Particle dynamics in rotating charged Bumblebee black holes: An analytical correspondence with Kerr-Newman

    Deyou Chen, Chuang Yang

    Shows that equatorial spinless and spinning test-particle motion in a slowly rotating charged Bumblebee black hole maps onto an effective Kerr-Newman spacetime. Circular-orbit radii and angular velocities match, while radial velocities and Lyapunov exponents pick up Lorentz-violation-dependent factors.

    arXiv:2611.04750gr-qchep-threlevance 6quality 6

    Rotating charged bumblebee black holes; Lorentz-violating gravity solutions.

  9. 9

    GUT Color-Triplet Around the Corner: The Story of an Overlooked Lightness

    Gia Dvali, Manuel Ettengruber, Anna Jankowsky, Tongxuan Zhang

    The paper builds explicit supersymmetric SO(10) models in which the light color-triplet Higgs partner couples to Standard Model fields only through supergravity-induced VEV shifts of order m_3/2/M_GUT or smaller. This gives lifetimes of about a second (Model I) or far beyond the age of the Universe (Model II), and the authors also argue the vacua obey no-de Sitter and Minkowski-safety criteria.

    arXiv:2611.03783hep-phhep-threlevance 3quality 7

    SO(10) GUT doublet-triplet splitting; phenomenology, with minor swampland-ish remarks.

  10. 10

    Scale-Dependent Weak-Field Dynamics in Horndeski Theory: Galaxy Rotation Curves and Turnaround Scales

    Hatice Özer

    Studies weak-field solutions of a gravitational-wave-compatible cubic Horndeski model with Vainshtein screening and derives rotation-curve modifications, gravitational slip, and a turnaround radius. The scalar effect is localized near the Vainshtein scale and does not give asymptotically flat rotation curves.

    arXiv:2611.05089gr-qcrelevance 6quality 5

    Horndeski weak-field dynamics and scalar-tensor modified gravity solutions.
