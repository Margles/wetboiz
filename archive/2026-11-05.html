  1. 1

    5-brane webs and generalised symmetries

    Mohammad Akhond, Federico Carta, Sung-Soo Kim, Futoshi Yagi

    The paper gives a microscopic prescription for reading 1-form symmetries off 5-brane webs, using D3-branes and (p,q) strings as Wilson lines whose screening goes through string junctions, Higgsing and Hanany-Witten transitions. It reproduces the Smith normal form for webs without orientifolds, proposes an effective charge rule for O7+ planes, and finds that for O5-planes the symmetry also depends on the local brane structure near the orientifold.

    arXiv:2611.06794hep-threlevance 10quality 8

    5-brane webs, 7-branes, orientifolds and 1-form symmetries; core 5D.

    Partially Massless Condensates and Reentrant NUT/Bolt Phase Transitions in Conformal Gravity

    Cristóbal Corral, Felipe Diaz, Borja Diez, Eleftherios Papantonopoulos, Per Sundell

    Studies Taub-NUT/Bolt instantons in conformal gravity with weakened AdS boundary conditions and finds a Bolt branch with a partially massless response at zero source. The thermodynamics shows reentrant NUT/Bolt transitions and possible negative thermodynamic volume.

    arXiv:2611.05787hep-threlevance 9quality 7

    Bach-flat conformal gravity NUT/Bolt instantons; directly matches a wanted paper.

  3. 3

    Inflationary attractors in metric-affine gravity

    Ilaria Andrei, Christian Dioguardi

    Studies slow-roll inflation in metric-affine gravity with non-dynamical torsion and non-metricity. At strong coupling it finds an alpha-attractor with n_s = 1 - 2/N and r = 12 alpha/N^2, where alpha = 1 + zeta/(6 xi), recovering Starobinsky and Palatini inflation as limits, and shows the structure persists without projective invariance.

    arXiv:2611.06750gr-qcastro-ph.COhep-phrelevance 9quality 7

    Inflationary attractors in metric-affine gravity; Starobinsky and Palatini limits, directly matching a wanted paper.

  4. 4

    Axial black hole perturbations in purely-metric Horndeski theories

    Héloïse Delaporte, Hugo Roussille

    The paper studies axial perturbations of curvaturised black holes in purely-metric Horndeski theories. It finds they propagate on an effective metric with the same causal structure as the background, are linearly stable, and computes the l=2 fundamental quasi-normal mode and first five overtones.

    arXiv:2611.06875gr-qcrelevance 7quality 8

    Black hole perturbations in Horndeski theories; hairy black holes and stability.

  5. 5

    An obstruction to cosmic acceleration from shift-symmetric dark sector interactions

    Jose Beltrán Jiménez, Luis J. Garay, Erik Jensko, María Pérez Garrote

    Using the effective field theory of a perfect fluid coupled to a superfluid, the paper derives ghost and gradient stability conditions for scalar perturbations. It concludes that slow-roll expansion is incompatible with stability, and that a stable phantom-divide crossing can only go from non-phantom to phantom, the opposite of the direction favoured by DESI.

    arXiv:2611.05560astro-ph.COgr-qchep-threlevance 6quality 7

    Obstruction to cosmic acceleration from dark sector interactions; reader wanted this paper.

    Higher Integrability, Generalized Symmetries, and Partially Topological Field Theories

    Luigi Alfonsi, Leron Borsten, Mehran Jalali Farahani, Hyungrok Kim, Martin Wolf, Charles A. S. Young

    The paper shows that in a non-Lorentzian higher semi-holomorphic Chern-Simons theory on M x CP^1, the equations of motion give a flat spectral-parameter-dependent higher Lax connection. Its Laurent coefficients give conserved quantities and classical line defects that realise a (d_M-2)-form symmetry, tested on gauge-invariant probes.

    arXiv:2611.06507hep-thmath-phrelevance 7quality 7

    Generalized symmetries with higher integrability; title wanted previously.

    Gravitational waves flooding the Universe after wall domination

    Sabir Ramazanov, Zhen-Min Zeng

    The paper studies an early-universe epoch of domain wall domination followed by wall annihilation into radiation. It estimates the resulting gravitational wave spectrum, which has a distinctive plateau and can raise N_eff above BBN limits unless the wall-dominated stage lasts fewer than about 10 e-folds.

    arXiv:2611.05504astro-ph.COhep-phhep-threlevance 7quality 7

    Gravitational waves from domain walls with tensor modes frozen outside the horizon; reader wanted this paper.

  8. 8

    Anomaly effective actions for 4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ superconformal field theories

    Pantelis Panopoulos, Ioannis Papadimitriou

    The paper constructs the Wess-Zumino anomaly effective action of 4d N=1 SCFTs in components via the coset construction, determining the superconformal anomalies of conformal supergravity in a two-parameter family of schemes. It finds a local counterterm that yields a generalized WZ action reproducing all anomalies and gives the component form of the superspace WZ action of old-minimal supergravity.

    arXiv:2611.06842hep-threlevance 6quality 8

    Weyl anomaly effective actions in conformal supergravity; reader wanted this paper.

    Quantum error correcting codes from quiver moduli: Grassmannians, cyclic monodromy, and argyle collision divisors

    Steven Rayan, Evan Sundbo

    Builds exact quantum error correcting codes from cyclic and argyle quiver moduli on the projective line, and proves the largest Kostka multiplicity is the optimal logical dimension for monodromy noise. Gives explicit codewords for a six-sheet qubit and examples of errors outside the noise model.

    arXiv:2611.05526quant-phhep-thmath.AGmath.COcross-listrelevance 6quality 6

    Quantum error correcting codes from quiver moduli; reader wanted this paper.

    Born-Infeld black holes in bumblebee gravity

    A. A. Araújo Filho, E. M. Siquieri, N. Heidari, V. B. Bezerra, A. F. Santos

    The paper constructs exact static spherical charged black hole solutions in bumblebee gravity coupled to Born-Infeld electrodynamics and studies horizons, geodesics, photon spheres, and shadows. It derives conditional bounds on the charge and nonlinear scale from Sgr A*, M87*, and planetary perihelion data.

    arXiv:2611.06659gr-qcrelevance 8quality 5

    Black hole solutions in bumblebee Lorentz-violating gravity; closely matches a wanted paper.

    Missing solutions in scalar-Gauss-Bonnet gravity

    Oğuzhan K. Yamak, Kalin V. Staykov, Fethi M. Ramazanoğlu, Daniela D. Doneva, Stoytcho S. Yazadjiev

    Shows that scalarized neutron-star branches in scalar-Gauss-Bonnet gravity that appear to terminate can reappear after a gap, and finds these previously missed solutions numerically. It identifies a new non-central regularity condition, the vanishing of the determinant of the radial evolution system, that explains missing solutions the known central condition cannot.

    arXiv:2611.06246gr-qcrelevance 6quality 7

    Missing solutions in scalar-Gauss-Bonnet gravity; same title was wanted.

    Gluon and Faddeev--Popov ghost propagators in rotating background: An eigenfunction approach

    Benjamín García-Contreras, Luis A. Hernández, R. Zamora

    Constructs the free gluon and Faddeev-Popov ghost propagators in Feynman gauge in rigidly rotating cylindrical coordinates via an eigenfunction expansion, recovering the Minkowski propagator without boundaries. It then extends the construction to a finite-radius cylinder with relative boundary conditions for gluons and Dirichlet for ghosts.

    arXiv:2611.05789hep-thhep-phrelevance 6quality 6

    Faddeev-Popov ghost propagators; wanted similar paper, gauge-fixing topic.

    Gravitational-Wave Constraints on $R^2$ Gravity

    Pawan Kumar Gupta, Laura Sagunski

    The paper maps quadratic f(R) gravity onto a massive scalar-tensor EFT inspiral waveform and fits GW170817 and GW190425. It finds a 90% bound a < 6.2e6 m^2, equivalent to a scalar mass above about 3e-11 eV.

    arXiv:2611.06902gr-qcastro-ph.HEhep-threlevance 6quality 6

    Gravitational-wave constraints on R^2 gravity; the reader previously wanted the same title.

  14. 14

    Bumblebee radiation from neutral matter around a black hole

    A. A. Araújo Filho

    Computes the vector (bumblebee) radiation sourced by a neutral point particle orbiting a black hole in the radial bumblebee vacuum, finding an axial dipole at the orbital frequency and a quadrupole at twice that for slightly eccentric orbits. It gives the dipole tidal strain and partial luminosity, scaling as e^2 v^12.

    arXiv:2611.05676gr-qchep-threlevance 6quality 5

    Bumblebee gravity radiation around a black hole; Lorentz-violating gravity.
