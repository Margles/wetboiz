  1. 1

    What Comes Out Of Monopole-Fermion Scattering?

    David Tong

    Studies massless chiral fermions scattering off a monopole in the s-wave using bosonisation and shows the outgoing state carries a soft cloud of infinitely many particle-antiparticle pairs, with n(ω)~1/ω. It computes energy and charge correlators in 3+1d, finding logarithmically growing charge fluctuations, and discusses mass deformations.

    arXiv:2611.17416hep-thhep-ph

    Monopole-fermion scattering, twisted sector; the reader wanted a similar paper.

  2. 2

    Microcausality Bounds on Dispersion Laws

    Paolo Creminelli, Michael Mancini, Warin Patrick McBlain

    The paper shows that microcausality (Im ω ≤ |Im k|) is equivalent to k ± ω(k) being typically real functions. This gives two-sided bounds on the higher-order coefficients of linear dispersion relations in terms of the radius of convergence, and these bounds shrink as c_s → 1. The bounds are tested on superfluid, optical, magnetized-photon, CFL and solid models, and extended to ω² and to gapped modes.

    arXiv:2611.17407hep-th

    Causality bounds on dispersion laws, EFT of inflation; positivity-type constraints.

  3. 3

    Black Hole Probes under T-duality

    Matilda Delgado, Dieter Lüst, Giulia Tazzoli, Matteo Zatti

    The paper T-dualizes 4d N=2 BPS attractor black holes along Euclidean time and a spatial circle to construct type IIB D-instantons and axionic strings, first on T^6/(Z2xZ2) and then on a generic Calabi-Yau threefold. It maps black-hole mass, entropy and warp factor to instanton action, measure and 4d dilaton, and finds that finite-size black holes all probe the emergent fundamental-string limit while small black holes can probe other emergent-string and decompactification limits.

    arXiv:2611.17413hep-th

    Calabi-Yau compactification moduli space limits, axionic strings, emergent string limits.

  4. 4

    Linear monitoring-feedback Model for Linearized gravity

    Daisuke Miki, Alfred Li, Yiqiu Ma, Yubao Liu, Yanbei Chen

    The paper builds a linear Heisenberg-picture monitoring-feedback framework in which ancillas monitor masses and feed records back to generate gravity. It derives a minimum classical-gravity noise for self-gravity, a determinant bound that forbids entanglement for local-record models, and shows that correlated ancilla inputs can reproduce linearized quantum-gravity noise and commutators.

    arXiv:2611.17642quant-phgr-qccross-list

    Tests of quantum nature of gravity via monitoring-feedback models.

  5. 5

    Geometric Quantization of the Giant Graviton Master Moduli Space

    Kasia Budzik, Davide Gaiotto

    The paper reorganizes finite-N U(N) gauge-invariant BPS operators as single-trace plus determinant operators modulo "open loop equations", interpreted as a gauged geometric quantization of the Giant Graviton moduli space. It builds BRST/BV complexes conjectured to categorify Murthy's Giant Graviton expansion, and verifies this with explicit cohomology computations in examples.

    arXiv:2611.18539hep-thmath-ph

    Giant graviton moduli with BRST complexes; only loosely related.

  6. 6

    Positivity Bounds, Negative Running, and the Weak Gravity Conjecture

    Jaime Fernandez

    The paper studies a photon-dilaton-fermion EFT in which a Yukawa coupling drives the four-photon F^4 coefficient negative in the IR. It shows this is consistent with one-loop positivity bounds because of the massless scalar's IR contribution, and that the induced fermion mass in the black-hole background blocks the negative running from making extremal black holes subextremal, so the Weak Gravity Conjecture is not violated.

    arXiv:2611.17408hep-phhep-th

    Positivity bounds and Weak Gravity Conjecture with dilaton black holes.

  7. 7

    Superstars, quantum wormholes and von Neumann algebras

    Vijay Balasubramanian, Ryo Nemoto, Masaki Shigemori

    The paper entangles heavy 1/2-BPS states in two copies of N=4 SYM, dual to singular superstar/hyperstar geometries, and determines the von Neumann algebra type of coarse and fine BPS probe algebras. Coarse probes give Type I (disconnected), while fine bosonic and fermionic probes give Type II∞ or III_λ (a quantum wormhole). The paper also notes a correspondence between algebra type and thermodynamic stability of the superstar.

    arXiv:2611.17404hep-th

    Wormholes and von Neumann algebras in AdS/CFT; loosely related to entanglement.

  8. 8

    Gravitational Corrections to OPE Coefficients

    Ameya Chavda, Alessandro Podo, John Staunton

    The paper computes leading-order curvature corrections to curved-space OPE coefficients using flat-space correlators with a stress-tensor insertion. It covers weakly coupled scalar theories and general CFTs, and uses the results to confirm Witten's time-smearing construction of the observer's algebra.

    arXiv:2611.18415hep-th

    QFT in curved spacetime OPE; no listed topic.

  9. 9

    Dynamical Gauss--Bonnet Sum Rules and Global Consistency in Five-Dimensional Braneworld Cosmology

    Paulo Michel Longo Tavares da Silva

    The paper derives an exact integrated trace consistency relation for 5D Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity with a canonical scalar on a periodic extra dimension. It uses the independent local equations to prove that static periodic warping excludes all expanding power-law FRW solutions, and it compares its exact contraction to a prior perturbative weighted construction.

    arXiv:2611.17663hep-thmath-ph

    Gauss-Bonnet five-dimensional braneworld cosmology with scalar field; extra dimensions.

  10. 10

    The Full-Shape Analysis of the Fading Dark Sector Model

    Georges Obied, Cumrun Vafa, David H. Wu

    The paper extends the string-motivated Fading Dark Sector model (a scalar coupling dark energy and dark matter, with a fifth force in the dark sector) to the DESI DR1 full-shape galaxy power spectrum using a model-specific EFTofLSS. Combined with CMB, DESI DR2 BAO and DES-Dovekie, it finds unchanged (c,c') values, Δχ²≈-4 relative to CPL, and a 4.1σ preference over ΛCDM.

    arXiv:2611.17422astro-ph.COhep-phhep-th

    Dark sector model fit to DESI data; observational focus.

  11. 11

    Axion quintessence with a negative cosmological constant: Bayesian constraints from current cosmological data

    Masroor C. Pookkillath, Gary Shiu, Shinji Tsujikawa

    The paper constrains axion quintessence with a negative cosmological constant using DES Y5 SN, DESI DR2 BAO, compressed CMB and growth data with nested sampling. It finds a preference for recent thawing but no compelling Bayesian evidence for the negative constant, and shows that the Weak Gravity Conjecture implies an unnaturally large instanton coefficient.

    arXiv:2611.17725astro-ph.COgr-qchep-th

    Axion quintessence data constraints; only WGC mention.

  12. 12

    Many-body Response to Spin-Independent and Spin-Dependent Dark Matter Scattering in a Superfluid Helium-3 Target

    A. Ting, N. Darvishi, S. Autti, L. Bloomfield, A. Casey, N. Eng, P. Franchini, R. P. Haley, P. J. Heikkinen, A. Jennings, A. Kemp, E. Leason + 16 more

    The paper computes spin-independent and spin-dependent light dark matter scattering in superfluid 3He using Fermi-gas structure functions, including Fermi-Dirac statistics and Pauli blocking. It finds that the single-atom approximation holds across most of the mass range, while the many-body treatment extends the low-mass reach by about 2.5% at a 0.51 eV threshold, with larger gains at lower thresholds.

    arXiv:2611.17432hep-phastro-ph.COhep-ex

    Dark matter detection in superfluid helium; out of scope.

  13. 13

    Resonant Trapping of Neutrinophilic Dark Matter in Supernovae

    Christopher V. Cappiello, P. S. Bhupal Dev, Amol V. Patwardhan

    The paper studies supernova cooling bounds on dark matter coupled to neutrinos through a chi-nu-phi interaction, where s-channel resonant scattering with antineutrinos traps the DM. Using a 1D PNS simulation, it finds that the SN1987A exclusion region has a gap for mediator masses of roughly 5-100 MeV, so the constraints are much weaker than for other interaction types.

    arXiv:2611.17604hep-phastro-ph.HE

    Dark matter supernova phenomenology; out of scope.
