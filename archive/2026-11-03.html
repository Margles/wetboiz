1. 5d SCFTs and Intersection Theory of Generalized Toric Threefolds
Hülya Argüz, Antoine Bourget, Pierrick Bousseau, Julius F. Grimminger, Sakura Schafer-Nameki · 2611.00366 [hep-th]
Develops intersection theory for generalized toric Calabi-Yau threefolds and gives an algorithm computing triple intersection numbers, hence the 5d prepotential, from generalized toric polygon data. Checks it on examples including SU(4) with an antisymmetric and 7 flavors, recovering the Chern-Simons level, and releases Mathematica code.
relevance 10 · quality 8 · 5d SCFTs, generalized toric threefolds prepotential; core.
2. Generalized Toric Threefolds from Scattering Diagrams and Broken Lines
Antoine Bourget, Ignacio Carreño Bolla, Quentin Lamouret · 2611.00206 [hep-th]
The paper gives an explicit scattering-diagram and broken-line description of generalized toric Calabi-Yau threefolds from 5-brane webs ending on 7-branes. It builds affine charts and gluing maps, and uses them to study Hanany-Witten moves, divisors, triple intersections and the Euler characteristic.
relevance 10 · quality 7 · Generalized toric threefolds, 5-brane webs with 7-branes; core.
3. Non-Invertible Charges for M-Theory
Kenneth Intriligator, Aiden Sheckler, Eric Y. Yang · 2611.00196 [hep-th]
The authors model the gauge charges of M2 and M5 branes as non-invertible charge defects of a 'pseudo 6-group'. They show that this model reproduces features such as Hanany-Witten moves, singleton decomposition of M5-brane worldvolume theories and M5/anti-M5 couplings.
relevance 7 · quality 8 · Non-invertible charges for M-theory branes; wanted in digest.
4. Dimensionally Regulated Graviton Self-Energy from Gravitons on de Sitter
B. Yesilyurt, N. C. Tsamis, R. P. Woodard · 2611.02120 [gr-qc, hep-th]
The paper computes the dimensionally regulated, BPHZ-renormalized one-loop graviton self-energy on de Sitter in a simple gauge. It uses it to correct the gravitational wave mode function and the Newtonian potential, finding that the potential grows like the cube of the number of e-foldings.
relevance 7 · quality 8 · Graviton self-energy on de Sitter; matches wanted paper.
5. A note on the quantization of M2-branes on local Calabi-Yau manifolds
Yutong Huang, Qinjian Lou, Jiahua Tian, Yi-Nan Wang · 2611.00687 [hep-th, math-ph]
Revisits the quantization of M2-branes wrapping curves in local Calabi-Yau threefolds from the supermembrane action, interpreting refined Gopakumar-Vafa invariants through the moduli space and Jacobian fibration, with examples such as generalized conifolds. It also sketches the CY4 case, where the quantum mechanics is of N=2B type, for genus-zero curves.
relevance 9 · quality 7 · M2-branes on local CY, 5d SCFT BPS states; core.
6. I want to Break Free: The Positive Side of Symmetry Breaking
Anubha Bal, Claudia de Rham · 2611.03510 [hep-th]
The paper classifies derivative operators of N-scalar EFTs by the internal symmetry subgroups of O(N) they preserve, and uses 2-2 and 3-3 positivity bounds on superposition states to show that coefficients of symmetry-breaking operators are bounded by those of symmetry-preserving ones. Examples include O(8) to U(4) and O(7) to G2 cascades.
relevance 5 · quality 8 · Positivity bounds in EFT; the reader wanted this paper before.
7. A Singularity Theorem for Quasi-Topological Gravities
Francesco Di Filippo · 2611.02190 [gr-qc]
Proves that spherical collapse with matter obeying the null energy condition leads to null geodesic incompleteness in the known quasi-topological gravities, provided h'(psi) > 0. Regular vacuum black holes in these theories therefore do not make collapse predictive.
relevance 8 · quality 6 · Singularity theorem for quasi-topological gravities; matches a wanted paper.
8. An obstruction to generalised discrete torsion in string theory
Carlo Angelantonj, Giorgio Leone, Diego Perugini · 2611.00209 [hep-th]
The paper shows that point-wise generalised discrete torsion in T^6/Z_2^2 and T^7/Z_2^3 orbifolds, though modular invariant at genus one, gives an inconsistent spectrum and breaks genus-two modular invariance. Only Vafa's standard discrete torsion survives.
relevance 6 · quality 8 · Discrete torsion obstruction in string orbifolds; wanted in digest.
9. Reconstructing string dualities through symmetries of moduli spaces
Ignacio Ruiz, Germán Varona · 2611.00235 [hep-th]
Uses the symmetries of moduli spaces together with the Emergent String Conjecture to constrain which duality frames can appear in asymptotic regions. For 32 and 16 supercharges this reproduces exactly the known toroidal compactifications, and for less SUSY it leaves a few unexplained extra possibilities.
relevance 6 · quality 8 · Moduli space dualities, emergent string conjecture; swampland-adjacent; wanted similar.
10. Black Hole Love Numbers with Physics-Informed Neural Networks
Maria J. Rodriguez, Luis Fernando Temoche, Pavlos Protopapas · 2611.02084 [gr-qc, cs.LG, hep-th, math.NA]
The authors train a physics-informed neural network with an H^2 Sobolev loss to solve the static Kerr perturbation equation and extract the tidal Love number from the asymptotic coefficients. They recover the dissipative response to relative errors below 1e-5 and compare eight extraction methods.
relevance 6 · quality 6 · Black hole Love numbers with PINNs; matches a wanted paper title.
11. Strong-coupling of Minkowski spacetime in $f(R)$ gravity: a Hamiltonian perspective
Aldair Pantoja · 2611.00413 [gr-qc]
Performs a Hamiltonian constraint analysis of f(R) gravity perturbed around Minkowski space and counts degrees of freedom for the degenerate and non-degenerate cases. It reproduces the known result that R0-degenerate models are strongly coupled with no propagating modes, while non-degenerate ones have 3 or 2.
relevance 8 · quality 5 · Hamiltonian analysis of f(R) gravity, constraints and degrees of freedom.
12. Thermal Festina Lente
Fayez Abu-Ajamieh, Nobuchika Okada, Roman Pasechnik, Victoria Venken, Zhi-Wei Wang · 2611.02559 [hep-th, hep-ph]
The paper extends the Festina Lente bound to a thermal bath, getting m greater than about l times (cV/sigma)^(1/4) with l logarithmic in the coupling. It applies this to charged particles, gauged B-L neutrino masses, millicharges, and inflation.
relevance 6 · quality 7 · Festina Lente swampland bound extension; the reader wanted 'Thermal Festina Lente' before.
13. Remark About Action for Rastall Gravity
J. Kluson · 2611.00136 [gr-qc]
Makes Golovnev's Rastall gravity action diffeomorphism invariant via deparametrization, introducing a dynamical vector tau^mu. The equations of motion then force constant scalar curvature, which restricts matter to traceless cases and shows the covariant action does not reproduce Rastall gravity in general.
relevance 6 · quality 5 · Action for Rastall gravity; matches wanted note on the same topic.
14. Quantum Black Holes in Non-Linear Electrodynamics
Jana Menšíková, David Kubiznak, Robert B. Mann · 2611.00808 [hep-th, gr-qc]
The paper builds charged quantum black holes on a 3D brane from a 4D bulk with RegMax nonlinear electrodynamics, deriving the new counterterms. It then checks the first law and shows the horizon structure can be Schwarzschild-like or Reissner-Nordstrom-like.
relevance 4 · quality 7 · Braneworld holography quantum black holes; similar to wanted quantum black holes paper.
15. Quantum superposition of Cosmic String spacetimes
Enrique Arias, Shoshauna Gauvin, Willy Izquierdo, Robert B. Mann · 2611.00327 [gr-qc, hep-th]
Computes the response of an Unruh-DeWitt detector coupled to a massless scalar field in a quantum superposition of two cosmic string spacetimes with integer topological charges N and M. Finds an interference term absent for a classical mixture, with resonances when the image structures of the two branches align.
relevance 7 · quality 5 · Superposition of cosmic string spacetimes as test of quantum gravity.
16. Exact Regular Planar Black Holes solutions in Bumblebee gravity
Daniil Stepanenko · 2611.03338 [gr-qc, hep-th]
The paper finds exact planar black hole solutions in bumblebee gravity with temporal and radial vector components, for a quadratic potential with zero cosmological constant and a linear potential with nonzero cosmological constant. The nonminimal coupling is fixed to 1/4 and the curvature invariants are finite at r=0.
relevance 6 · quality 5 · Regular black hole solutions in bumblebee gravity; matches wanted bumblebee papers.
17. Distance to integrability and complexity
Sean Casey, Marius de Leeuw, Tristan McLoughlin, Sergiy Zhuk · 2611.00233 [quant-ph, cond-mat.stat-mech, cond-mat.str-el, hep-th, math-ph, nlin.SI] (cross-list)
The authors classify the 12 families of integrable translation-invariant nearest-neighbour spin-1/2 chains and numerically define a distance from any Hamiltonian to the nearest integrable one. They show that level-spacing statistics and eigenstate entropy interpolate between integrable and chaotic values as this distance grows, with large model-to-model variation, and that the distance constrains early-time Krylov complexity.
relevance 1 · quality 7 · Integrability distance in spin chains; unrelated.
