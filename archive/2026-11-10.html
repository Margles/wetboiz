    The Ghost and Its Secret Partner

    Luca Buoninfante

    Argues that the complex-conjugate poles of a ghost propagator above the multi-particle threshold imply a hidden asymptotic composite state coupled to the ghost. Applies this to quadratic gravity, where the spin-two ghost cannot appear as an isolated asymptotic state.

    arXiv:2611.10128hep-thgr-qcrelevance 10quality 7

    Ghost in quadratic gravity, indefinite-norm states, unitarity.

  2. 2

    An RG perspective on the (un)naturalness of Starobinsky inflation

    Francesca Campani, Francesco Ferrarin, Serena Giardino, Alessia Platania

    The paper follows asymptotically safe f(R) RG trajectories from the UV fixed point down to inflationary scales to test whether a large R^2 coupling implies equally large higher-order couplings. It finds comparable dimensionless couplings in the UV but canonical suppression of higher-order terms at sub-Planckian scales, leaving small calculable deviations from Starobinsky predictions.

    arXiv:2611.10326hep-threlevance 10quality 7

    Starobinsky inflation R^2 naturalness via asymptotic safety RG.

  3. 3

    Direct product geometries in $n$-dimensional linear, quadratic, and conformal gravity

    Petr Jizba, Tereza Lehečková

    The paper classifies direct-product spacetimes of (m1+m2), (2+m) and (2+...+2) type in Einstein gravity, pure quadratic gravity and a higher-dimensional conformal gravity, allowing nonconstant curvature in the 2D factors. It finds that the allowed geometries depend on the theory and on the quadratic couplings, and that standard static black holes are absent from the direct-product sector of this conformal gravity for n>4.

    arXiv:2611.12483gr-qchep-thmath-phrelevance 9quality 7

    Exact solutions in quadratic and conformal gravity.

  4. 4

    On Thermodynamic Role of the Gauss-Bonnet Topological Term

    Marek Liška, David Kubiznak

    The paper revisits whether the 4D Gauss-Bonnet topological term shifts black hole entropy, showing the answer depends on the choice of horizon boundary action and that a suitable choice makes the Wald entropy reproduce the Jacobson-Myers entropy. It also shows the term changes the Hawking-Page critical temperature and, for positive coupling, can make the transition zeroth-order at high pressure.

    arXiv:2611.12698hep-thgr-qcrelevance 6quality 8

    Gauss-Bonnet term in 4D gravity, entropy and Hawking-Page thermodynamics.

  5. 5

    Cubic Curvature Corrections to Taub-NUT-AdS Black Holes and Their Thermodynamics

    Yu-Qi Chen, Hai-Shan Liu, Hong Lu

    Constructs the first-order cubic-curvature corrected Taub-NUT-AdS black hole and derives its temperature and Wald entropy. The Gibbs free energy, mass and NUT charge are fixed by two improved Reall-Santos methods, which agree.

    arXiv:2611.12482hep-thgr-qcrelevance 6quality 7

    Cubic curvature corrections to Taub-NUT-AdS black holes and thermodynamics.

  6. 6

    Scale-separated quasi-dS and the Swampland

    Rafael Álvarez-García, Ivano Basile, Shi Chen, Cumrun Vafa

    Tests whether scale-separated quasi-de Sitter solutions can be controlled in M-theory compactifications on hyperbolic and rigid Riemann-flat manifolds once Casimir energy is included. Finds that the potential slope cannot fall below the TCC value within EFT control, except possibly in an order-one bulk region where control is lost.

    arXiv:2611.10116hep-threlevance 5quality 8

    Swampland dS conjecture/TCC in M-theory compactifications.

  7. 7

    Nonlinear Scalarization of Reissner-Nordström Black Holes in Scalar-Torsion-Maxwell Teleparallel Gravity

    Joaquín Robledo, Yerko Vásquez, P. A. González

    The paper studies nonlinear scalarization of Reissner-Nordstrom black holes in scalar-torsion-Maxwell teleparallel gravity with quartic and sixth-order couplings. It constructs numerically hairy charged black holes, classifies them by scalar node number, and finds that the higher-order coupling suppresses higher-node branches.

    arXiv:2611.11686gr-qchep-threlevance 8quality 6

    Scalar-torsion teleparallel gravity black hole scalarization solutions.

  8. 8

    Emergent de Sitter Universes in Non-Hermitian Holography

    Daniel Areán, Suman Das, David Garcia-Fariña, Juan F. Pedraza

    The authors construct asymptotically AdS black holes in PT-symmetric non-Hermitian holography whose interiors bounce and approach an expanding de Sitter phase behind a single horizon. They relate AdS boundary observables (stress tensor, two-sided correlators, complexity, shockwaves) to dS/CFT and static-patch holography.

    arXiv:2611.10121hep-thgr-qcrelevance 7quality 7

    Non-Hermitian holography with emergent de Sitter interiors.

  9. 9

    Einstein--Yang--Mills Plane Waves: Uniqueness and an Exact Curvature Double Copy

    Metin Gurses, Tahsin Cagri Sisman, Bayram Tekin

    The paper studies Coleman's non-abelian Yang-Mills plane waves coupled to gravity in D dimensions. It shows the linear-profile wave is the unique one with bounded energy density and the unique one whose Riemann tensor equals the color-squared field strength, builds AdS families, and shows spherical transverse spaces give singular solutions.

    arXiv:2611.12204hep-thgr-qcrelevance 6quality 8

    Einstein-Yang-Mills plane waves, exact solutions in quadratic and Gauss-Bonnet gravity.

  10. 10

    The Gribov problem on the horizon: A short guide to the Gribov-Zwanziger framework and its refinement

    Antonio D. Pereira

    A short review of Gribov copies in Landau-gauge Yang-Mills theory, the Gribov-Zwanziger action, and its refinement with dimension-two condensates, which gives decoupling-type propagators matching lattice data. It also surveys applications and open questions.

    arXiv:2611.10303hep-thhep-lathep-phrelevance 7quality 6

    Gribov problem and Faddeev-Popov gauge fixing review.

    ERG, axions, and a PT-symmetric accelerating Universe

    Nick E. Mavromatos, Sarben Sarkar

    Conjectures that singularities in ERG beta functions for axion-photon couplings signal a transition to a PT-symmetric gravity phase with repulsive Newton constant. They suggest this could drive late-time acceleration without dark energy.

    arXiv:2611.10584gr-qchep-threlevance 7quality 5

    PT-symmetric gravity with axions and Chern-Simons terms, accelerating universe.

  12. 12

    Scalar-tensor reconstruction of coherent quantum black holes

    R. Casadio, A. Giusti, R. da Rocha

    The paper reconstructs the scalar-tensor and Horndeski actions that reproduce a Gaussian-smeared coherent-state Schwarzschild geometry. It finds the kinetic term is positive only in finite radial domains, and that neither reconstruction covers the horizon or arbitrarily large parts of the interior and exterior.

    arXiv:2611.10616gr-qchep-threlevance 6quality 6

    Scalar-tensor and Horndeski reconstruction of quantum-corrected black holes.

  13. 13

    Quality time in extra dimensions: axion quality and radion cosmology

    Falguni Biswas, Yeshan Juberi, Ahmed Rakin Kamal

    The paper studies axion quality in a five-dimensional circle compactification with a dynamical radius stabilised by Casimir energy. It finds Minkowski and de Sitter minima with residual strong CP angle around 1e-17, then works out radion decay and reheating and how the resulting entropy dilutes the axion abundance.

    arXiv:2611.12765hep-phastro-ph.COhep-threlevance 6quality 6

    Five-dimensional circle compactification, radion, higher-form symmetries; mostly axion phenomenology though.

  14. 14

    Gravitational waves and the exit from de Sitter in Inflation without an Inflaton

    Daniele Bertacca, Raul Jimenez, Sabino Matarrese, Mariam Abdelaziz, Marisol Traforetti

    The paper examines second-order graviton-induced curvature in de Sitter and finds that tensor modes do not receive the same enhancement as the scalar. It then follows the curvature through an exit into radiation domination, assuming a global clock, and predicts 31 < N_w < 50 with tensor-to-scalar ratio between 3e-6 and 0.036.

    arXiv:2611.12772astro-ph.COgr-qchep-threlevance 6quality 5

    Primordial tensor spectrum and gravitational waves in graviton-driven inflation; r predictions.

    Magnetohydrodynamic spectra and line invariants in the presence of axions

    P. M. Akhmetiev, M. S. Dvornikov, A. Yu. Smirnov
