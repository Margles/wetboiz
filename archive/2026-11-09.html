1. 5d $\mathbb{Z}_2$ S-folds and Seiberg--Witten curves
Sung-Soo Kim, Song Liu, Mudassar Sabir, Futoshi Yagi · 2611.09804 [hep-th]
The paper computes Seiberg-Witten curves for Z2 S-folds of 5d rank-1 SCFTs in two ways: by identifying points on the parent curve, and by tuning masses to insert D4 7-branes. The two curves agree in Weierstrass normal form for all four examples.
relevance 9 · quality 7 · 5d SCFT Seiberg-Witten curves, S-folds, 7-branes.
2. Dynamical gluon mass generation, the Schwinger mechanism, spontaneous symmetry breaking, and color confinement in the linear covariant gauges of QCD
Giorgio Comitini · 2611.09631 [hep-th, hep-ph]
Re-examines the Kugo-Ojima confinement criterion in linear covariant gauges and argues that its Goldstone equation was never correctly normalized. Argues the BRST quartet structure contains the Schwinger mechanism for gluon mass generation, realized as a Higgs-type breaking of the global gauge charge, compatible with unbroken color symmetry and confinement.
relevance 8 · quality 6 · Kugo-Ojima BRST quartet mechanism, unitarity of covariant gauges, confinement.
3. Type IIB Forms and Scalars from an $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{R})$ Topological Field Theory with Boundary
Euihun Joung, J. A. Rosabal · 2611.08841 [hep-th]
The authors build an eleven-dimensional SL(2,R) topological field theory with boundary whose edge modes are the type IIB axion-dilaton and form fields. The boundary equations give the IIB duality relations including F5 self-duality, and S-duality monodromies appear as holonomies of a flat connection.
relevance 6 · quality 8 · IIB axion-dilaton SL(2,R) formulation with S-duality monodromies as holonomies.
4. Equatorial Periodic Orbits and Gravitational Wave Signatures in Weyl Geometric Gravity Black Holes
Dhruba Jyoti Gogoi, Himanshu Chaudhary, Tiberiu Harko · 2611.08952 [gr-qc, hep-th]
Studies timelike geodesics, periodic orbits and numerical-kludge EMRI waveforms around a Weyl geometric gravity black hole. Finds that the nonmetricity parameters shift orbital lobes and waveform phases.
relevance 8 · quality 5 · Weyl geometric gravity black holes with nonmetricity; modified gravity solutions.
5. Fermionic Kaluza-Klein Reductions
Francesco Bertucci · 2611.08584 [hep-th, gr-qc]
Derives the Kaluza-Klein reduction of free massless spin-1/2 and spin-3/2 fields on a product spacetime M x N at the level of the action. The result is towers of massive fields with masses set by Dirac and Rarita-Schwinger eigenvalues on N, and the paper discusses the fate of gauge invariance.
relevance 6 · quality 7 · Kaluza-Klein reductions of spin-1/2 and 3/2 fields on product spaces.
6. AdS to dS Flows Dual to Pseudo-Hermitian Theories
José M. Begines, Antón F. Faedo, Carlos Hoyos, Daniele Musso · 2611.08587 [hep-th]
Constructs black-brane solutions of Einstein gravity coupled to a ghost scalar that interpolate between asymptotically AdS and dS regions across a horizon. They are interpreted as duals of pseudo-Hermitian theories, with one family from a supergravity truncation uplifted to eleven dimensions, and the paper studies thermodynamic and linear stability.
relevance 6 · quality 7 · Gravity with ghost scalar dual to pseudo-Hermitian theories; holography.
7. Discreetly Conformal: Higher-Order Chiral Scalars and the Discrete Series
Calvin Y. -R. Chen, Kurt Hinterbichler, Samanta Saha, Thomas Yan · 2611.08581 [hep-th]
The paper shows that a 2d massless scalar with higher shift symmetries gauged decomposes into pairs of higher-order chiral scalars, which realize a hidden conformal symmetry. It extends this to de Sitter, explaining the conformal symmetry of discrete series scalars.
relevance 2 · quality 8 · Higher-order chiral scalars and dS discrete series; not listed topics.
8. Challenges for minimal warm inflation in the Standard Model and beyond
Anish Ghoshal, Alessandro Strumia · 2611.09893 [hep-ph, astro-ph.CO]
The paper argues that minimal warm inflation with a phi G Gtilde coupling to QCD needs temperatures near the effective-operator cutoff, T/f of about 50-90, and a chemical potential far beyond the range where linear-response friction was tested. A UV completion needs an anomaly coefficient of about 100 or more, giving a sub-Planckian QCD Landau pole, and a more strongly coupled dark gauge sector relaxes these tensions.
relevance 2 · quality 8 · Warm inflation in the Standard Model; no listed topic, only loosely inflationary.
9. Thermal Field Theory for General Equilibrium Density Matrices
Alberto Salvio · 2611.09483 [hep-ph, hep-th]
Reviews thermal field theory for general equilibrium density matrices including rotation and chemical potentials, with path-integral derivations of partition functions and thermal propagators for scalars, fermions and gauge fields.
relevance 2 · quality 7 · Thermal field theory review; no listed topic.
