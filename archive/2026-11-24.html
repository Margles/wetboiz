    Central charges as black-hole hair in weakly coupled trace-anomaly gravity

    Francisco Tello-Ortiz, A. Vásquez, Kazuharu Bamba, Ghulam Mustafa

    The paper studies black holes in Gabadadze's weakly coupled trace-anomaly gravity with both Euler ($a$) and Weyl ($c$) central charges. It proves that constant-anomalyon Ricci-flat solutions exist iff $a=c$, derives the scalar hair charge in terms of $a-c$, surface gravity and charge (including sign reversal for charged black holes and divergence at the Cauchy horizon), and shows that perturbations after order reduction depend only on $a-c$.

    arXiv:2611.30708gr-qchep-thquality 8

    Black hole hair in trace-anomaly gravity with Weyl operator, ghost-freedom.

  2. 2

    Primordial black holes production in Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity

    Ekaterina O. Pozdeeva, Sergey Yu. Vernov

    The paper builds a single-field inflationary model with a scalar-field-dependent Gauss-Bonnet coupling $\xi(\phi)$ that has an ultra-slow-roll phase producing PBHs, while remaining consistent with ACT constraints on $n_s$ and $r$. Numerical integration shows a scalar power spectrum enhancement, positive scalar and tensor sound speeds squared, and PBH masses that could make up all or part of dark matter, at the cost of fine-tuning $\xi_0$.

    arXiv:2611.28150gr-qcastro-ph.COhep-thquality 8

    Gauss-Bonnet coupled inflation producing primordial black holes.

  3. 3

    Bring Your Own Instanton: Monopole Catalysis from Sourced SU(2) Instantons

    Csaba Csáki, Larissa Kiriliuk, Zhihan Liu, Rotem Ovadia

    The paper shows that Rubakov-Callan monopole catalysis in Georgi-Glashow $SU(2)$ theory is mediated by fermion-sourced non-Abelian instantons with $Q=1$, matching the count of fermion zero modes. It derives a closed-form correlator and amplitude that saturates unitarity as $E^{-1}$ for $E\ll g^2 m_W$ and is suppressed as $(g^2 m_W/E)^5 E^{-1}$ above that scale, which implies the process is governed by IR dynamics outside the core.

    arXiv:2611.28208hep-thhep-phquality 9

    Monopole catalysis via instantons; monopoles are tangential.

  4. 4

    Wormholes do not contribute to supersymmetric partition functions

    Jan Boruch, Douglas Stanford

    The paper argues that any connected wormhole spontaneously breaks one-sided (bosonic or fermionic) boundary symmetries, so for supersymmetry the integral over each orbit vanishes and wormholes do not contribute to products of supersymmetric partition functions. It also shows in SYK-like models that supersymmetric quantities can still have nonzero ensemble variance via non-geometric replica-correlated saddles that preserve all supersymmetry.

    arXiv:2611.28210hep-thquality 9🤖

    Wormholes and supersymmetric partition functions; gravitational path integral, tangential.

  5. 5

    ACT Excited: Reconciling Starobinsky and Quadratic Chaotic Inflationary Models with ACT Results via Excited States

    Amjad Ashoorioon, Abdolreza Yousefi-Sostani

    The paper shows that scale-dependent non-Bunch-Davies Bogoliubov excitations of scalar modes can shift $n_s$ and $\alpha_s$ to the ACT DR6 values for Starobinsky and quadratic chaotic inflation without changing the potential. It maps the allowed $(\varphi_S,\chi_S)$ regions under the $r$ bound and the $\Lambda>H$ backreaction condition, and finds that the quadratic model needs $\chi_S\gtrsim0.63$ and $\Lambda\lesssim6H$, with a violated consistency relation $r=-8n_T/\gamma_S$.

    arXiv:2611.28144astro-ph.COhep-thquality 8

    Starobinsky inflation vs ACT, tensor consistency relation; excited initial states.

  6. 6

    Rotating Black Holes from Scaling Transformations in Horava Gravity

    Deniz O. Devecioglu, Mu-In Park

    The paper shows that scaling the lapse and shift, $N\to\alpha N$, $N_i\to\beta N_i$, maps solutions of low-energy Hořava gravity to solutions with rescaled couplings, and uses this to build rotating black holes from Kerr in Doran and Kerr-Schild forms. The resulting solutions are horizon-penetrating with non-Killing null horizons and new curvature singularities that hide the causality-violating region, so chronology is protected.

    arXiv:2611.30337hep-thgr-qcquality 8

    Rotating black holes in Horava gravity, Lorentz-violating gravity solutions.

  7. 7

    For whom the Bell tolls: joint measurements and state updates in quantum field theory

    Christopher J. Fewster, Christiane K. M. Klein

    The paper derives the joint moment generating functional and the unique joint probability distribution for commuting observables $A(f_i)^*A(f_i)$ of a free complex field on Fock space, covering the Gamma and Kibble distributions and a Laguerre series for $m>2$. It uses this to build a Bell-test model in the Fewster-Verch framework with a coupled Proca-scalar system, showing CHSH violation approaching Tsirelson's bound when vacuum effects are rejected.

    arXiv:2611.30468math-phgr-qcquant-phcross-listquality 9

    Bell tests and measurement in QFT; weak quantum information link.

  8. 8

    Charged spin-$\frac32$ in a chiral Feshbach--Villars model: hyperbolic propagation, Landau-level instability, and exceptional points

    Haryanto M. Siahaan

    The paper studies a Pauli-coupled second-order wave equation on $(\frac32,0)\oplus(0,\frac32)$ with free gyromagnetic ratio $g$, linearized by a Feshbach-Villars transformation into a pseudo-Hermitian system. It derives the exact Landau spectrum (real for all fields iff $|g|j\le1$), finds a second-order exceptional point at $|qB|=m^2/2$ for $g=2$, and shows that a bounded electric perturbation produces complex eigenvalues for $m^2/6<|qB|<m^2/2$, confirmed by finite-basis numerics.

    arXiv:2611.30225hep-thquality 7🤖

    Pseudo-Hermitian Feshbach-Villars spin-3/2 with exceptional points; instability analysis.

  9. 9

    The index from a super-point

    Dhanvarsh Annamalai, Hyunook Hur, Thomas Hussel, Monica Jinwoo Kang, Craig Lawrie, Jaewon Song

    Extends the proposal that filtered graded rings and their arc spaces reproduce the Macdonald index of 4d $\mathcal{N}=2$ SCFTs to $\mathbb{Z}_2$-graded-commutative rings with fermionic generators. It treats the free vector multiplet, $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM with $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ and $\mathfrak{su}(3)$, rank-one $\mathcal{N}=3$ SCFTs, and $\widehat{E}_6(SU(2))$. For $\widehat{E}_6(SU(2))$ it finds that $\mathcal{A}(6)$ is not classically free, and that three extra relations in the first jet ring reproduce the index to order $q^{51}$.

    arXiv:2611.28204hep-thmath.AGmath.RTquality 9

    Arc spaces and Macdonald index for 4d N=2 SCFTs; not 5D.

    M\"obius Fusion Algebra

    Ippo Orii, Yuji Tachikawa

    Constructs a semisimple, generally non-commutative "Möbius fusion algebra" $\mathfrak{M}$ from a pseudo-unitary modular tensor category with an anti-monoidal involution $\mathsf{P}$, using ribbon graphs on the Möbius band. Shows its simple-module dimensions equal $M_X=\sum_Y S_{X,Y}\eta_Y$, proving this quantity is a non-negative integer, and verifies the result on explicit examples.

    arXiv:2611.29234math.QAhep-thmath-phmath.GTcross-listquality 10

    Modular tensor category mathematics on non-orientable TQFT; no listed topic.

    TeV-Scale Leptogenesis in the Linear Seesaw Framework

    A. E. Cárcamo Hernández, J. Echeverria-Puentes, Stephen F. King, José W. F. Valle

    The paper studies resonant leptogenesis in the minimal linear seesaw with one quasi-Dirac heavy neutrino pair at the TeV scale and a second leptophilic scalar doublet. It estimates the baryon asymmetry in flavoured, strong-washout regimes, finds viable parameter regions and benchmarks both near and away from the resonant maximum, and discusses complementary probes.

    arXiv:2611.28224hep-phquality 8

    Leptogenesis in linear seesaw; neutrino phenomenology.

  12. 12

    Higgs-portal dark matter beyond kinetic equilibrium

    Giorgio Arcadi, Michele Benaco, Mattia Di Mauro, Sami Nurmi, Kimmo Tuominen

    The paper solves the full momentum-dependent Boltzmann equation with DRAKE for scalar, vector and Dirac-fermion Higgs-portal dark matter near the Higgs resonance, and compares with the standard momentum-averaged treatment. It finds the relic abundance below resonance is underestimated by the standard method, by up to a factor of 10 in the coupling for pseudoscalar fermions and about 2 for scalar and vector, which strengthens the direct-detection constraints.

    arXiv:2611.28230hep-phastro-ph.COquality 8

    Higgs-portal dark matter relic abundance; out of scope.

    A hybrid effective field theory description of diffuse baryons

    Ryan Raikman, Kiyoshi Masui, James M. Sullivan, Josh Borrow

    The paper models the diffuse baryon (electron) density field with a hybrid EFT that uses the dark-matter-only $N$-body displacement field, a Lorentzian feedback suppression $c_f^2$, and a linear bias $b_{1,d}^L$. Two parameters fit the FLAMINGO diffuse gas power spectrum to $\mathcal{O}(1\%)$ up to $k=1\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}$ at $z=0.5$ and $k=1.5\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}$ at $z=1$, and higher-order bias terms are found to contribute negligibly.

    arXiv:2611.31069astro-ph.COquality 8🤖

    Baryon effective field theory for large-scale structure.

    Scotogenic dark matter from gauged $L_\mu+L_\tau$

    J. Echeverria-Puentes, Sergey Kovalenko, Pablo San Martin Sartori, Carlos A. Vaquera-Araujo, Yoxara Villamizar, Jilberto Zamora Saa

    The paper builds a scotogenic neutrino-mass model with gauged $U(1)_{L_\mu+L_\tau}$, anomaly-cancelling exotic leptons, and a remnant matter parity $M_P$ that stabilises scalar dark matter. It studies the thermal relic abundance for a singlet-like CP-even scalar, electroweak precision and kinetic-mixing constraints up to $\epsilon\sim10^{-2}$, and collider limits on the $Z'$.

    arXiv:2611.28223hep-phhep-thquality 7

    Scotogenic dark matter model; particle phenomenology.

    Quantifying the Impact of Galactic Dark Matter Halo Models on LZ Results

    Jialiang Shao, Andrea Addazi, Xinran Wang, Guanwen Fang, Yi-Fu Cai

    The paper rescales published LZ spin-independent and spin-dependent limits using 22 self-consistent halo models (isotropic, Osipkov-Merritt, axisymmetric, triaxial) and finds limits within about $\pm30\%$ of the isotropic reference above 40 GeV, but up to an order of magnitude different below 15 GeV. It also shows that the high-velocity tail controls the accessible mass splitting for an inelastic (Higgsino-like) interpretation of the 248 keV candidate event.

    arXiv:2611.29197hep-phastro-ph.COquality 7🤖

    Dark matter direct detection halo models; out of scope.
