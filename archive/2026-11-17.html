  1. 1

    Generalised global symmetries of 6d SCFTs on a circle from the blow-up equations

    William Harding, Noppadol Mekareeya

    The paper uses blow-up equations of 5d frames of 6d (1,0) SCFTs on a circle to build explicit 6d–5d frame maps, including the KK-to-instanton mass ratio r. It reads off 1-form symmetries, mixed anomalies and 2-groups from the exceptional-curve fluxes, for su(4) frames and SU(2) gaugings of E1 copies.

    arXiv:2611.18837hep-th

    6d SCFTs on a circle, 5d descriptions, higher-form symmetries and 2-groups.

  2. 2

    On the Spontaneous symmetry breaking of Conformal Gravity to Weyl and Einstein Gravities

    Partha Nandi, Gerasimos Kouniatalis, Stelios Stefas, George Zoupanos

    The paper revisits SO(2,4) conformal gauge gravity with an adjoint 15-plet Higgs and keeps the special-conformal connection as an independent field. On a source-free branch its algebraic equation gives the Schouten tensor and reduces the action to Weyl gravity, and the connection stays auxiliary in the full Yang-Mills-Higgs theory. It also finds massless and partially massless spin-2 branches on de Sitter at m^2=2Λ/3 and discusses a second breaking via a 6-plet that leads to Einstein gravity.

    arXiv:2611.19338hep-th

    Conformal gravity SSB to Weyl gravity; Weyl-squared action, partially massless spin-2.

  3. 3

    Black holes in Myrzakulov $F(R,T)$ gravity

    Alexandre Landry, Ratbay Myrzakulov

    The paper studies vacuum black holes in metric-affine Myrzakulov F(R,T)=αR+βT+γT^3 gravity. It finds an axial-torsion branch where the metric is Schwarzschild-(A)dS with torsionful connections, compares Ricci-symmetric connection families by their affine curvature invariants, and notes null directions of the restricted quadratic form without claiming stability.

    arXiv:2611.19500gr-qchep-thmath-ph

    Black holes in metric-affine torsionful F(R,T) gravity.

  4. 4

    Local field shifts and the Gribov-Zwanziger horizon functional

    Daniel Guimaraes Tedesco

    The paper constructs a Maggiore-Schaden-like local field re-centering of the Gribov-Zwanziger action using a compactly supported profile. It shows the horizon functional is unchanged at fixed background and finite cutoff under stated coercivity assumptions, analyzes source visibility of near-horizon Faddeev-Popov modes including non-self-adjoint compressions, and checks the tree-level RGZ propagator and one-loop gap equation.

    arXiv:2611.19513hep-th

    Gribov-Zwanziger horizon functional; Gribov problem and gauge fixing.

  5. 5

    Off-shell one-loop counterterms of Palatini gravity with an independent background connection

    F. T. Brandt, G. A. Menezes

    The paper computes the off-shell one-loop counterterms of first-order (Palatini) pure gravity in the background field method with an independent background connection. It gives 2-, 3- and 4-point results, 166 coefficients including nonmetricity invariants up to fourth order, checks Ward identities, and confirms that the counterterms vanish on shell and can be removed by field redefinitions.

    arXiv:2611.20295hep-thgr-qc

    One-loop counterterms in Palatini gravity with nonmetricity; metric-affine and perturbative gravity.

  6. 6

    Gravitational collapse in Lorentz-violating gravity and the spin-0 mode

    Emma Albertini, Massimo Vaglio, Enrico Barausse

    The paper evolves spherical scalar-field collapse in Einstein-aether theory numerically and shows that the spin-0 mode radiates as a 1/r wave at its linear-theory speed and carries positive energy to infinity. It also finds negative-energy static aether around the black hole, and, at large couplings, steepening collapse with no trapped surface that may signal a naked singularity. The authors discuss why this conflicts with the Taherasghari-Will post-Newtonian result.

    arXiv:2611.21034gr-qc

    Lorentz-violating Einstein-Aether/Horava gravity, spin-0 mode, collapse.

  7. 7

    $Q$-operators, cluster mutations, and flavored Toda chain

    Saebyeok Jeong, Taro Kimura, Norton Lee

    The paper builds Baxter Q-operators for a family of flavored q-deformed Toda chains from quantum cluster mutation sequences, and proves they commute. It identifies the associated transfer matrices with products of explicit Lax matrices and shows that cluster transformations move the flavor positions. It also derives integral kernels and a factorized scalar Q-function, which it compares with perturbative codimension-two defect contributions in 5d N=1 gauge theories.

    arXiv:2611.19436math-phhep-thcross-list

    Relates quivers to BPS quivers of 5d N=1 theories, codimension-two defects.

  8. 8

    Fourier Transform of the Scalar Propagator with Quantum Light-Cone Fluctuations

    M. V. Takook

    The paper computes the Lorentzian Fourier transform of a Gaussian-smeared light-cone term in the Krein-space scalar propagator, expressing it with a Meijer G-function, and shows how it combines with the Bessel tail to give the massive propagator. It then uses a position-space prescription to obtain an exact massless one-loop bubble kernel.

    arXiv:2611.18703gr-qc

    Krein-space quantization scalar propagator with light-cone fluctuations; indefinite metric.

  9. 9

    On Chen's Extended Action for $\kappa(R,T)$ Gravity: Gravity in a Polarizable Medium, Material Folds and a No-Bounce Theorem

    Ginés R. Pérez Teruel

    The paper uses Chen's adjoint action for κ(R,T) gravity to do a Dirac reduction of flat FLRW. It shows the reduced Hamiltonian is independent of κ(T) and that matter is non-conserved, with a dressed number conserved instead. For linear coupling with dust it finds a material fold at ρ_c/2 instead of a bounce, proves a no-bounce theorem for κ>0, and compares the model with effective LQC.

    arXiv:2611.21093gr-qc

    Modified gravity kappa(R,T) action, Hamiltonian Dirac analysis, no-bounce.

  10. 10

    Lean-Verified No-Go Theorems for $R$-Symmetries and Modular Symmetries in 4D GUTs

    Mu-Chun Chen, Xiang-Gan Liu, Michael Ratz, Alexander Stewart

    The paper formalizes in Lean 4 the no-go theorem forbidding an exact MSSM vacuum with an unbroken R-symmetry (Z_M^R with M≥3, or U(1)_R) in SU(5) GUTs, and closes a gap in the general case of the published proof. It then argues that modular symmetries are R-symmetries in SUGRA, so exotics become massless at symmetric critical points in moduli space, which may affect gauge coupling unification.

    arXiv:2611.20386hep-phhep-th

    R-symmetry no-go for GUTs; weak link to string/modular symmetries.

  11. 11

    Hunting dark matter: the present and future of direct particle detection

    Felix Kahlhoefer, Belina von Krosigk, Teresa Marrodán Undagoitia, Valentina Novati, María Luisa Sarsa

    Review of direct dark matter particle detection: the theory of scattering and absorption signatures, detector technologies, calibration and backgrounds, and experimental results since 2020. It also covers future experiments and the neutrino-fog challenge.

    arXiv:2611.21052hep-exastro-ph.COcross-list

    Dark matter direct detection review; out of scope.
