    Perturbative $S$-matrix Unitarity in Higher-Derivative Theory of Fermions

    Yugo Abe, Takeo Inami, Keisuke Izumi, Shinsuke Kawai

    The authors build a second-order-derivative fermion theory with quartic four-Fermi interactions. They split it into a normal fermion and a ghost fermion with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$, and show that the ghost necessarily has negative norm. They then compute tree-level amplitudes and find that they satisfy $S$-matrix tree-unitarity at high energy, in contrast to ordinary four-Fermi theory.

    Dark Energy Based on Modified Gravity: Where the Future Lies?

    Shin'ichi Nojiri, Sergei D. Odintsov, V. K. Oikonomou, Oem Trivedi

    The paper shows that realistic $F(R)$ dark energy models compatible with DESI data can evolve into anti-gravity or a ghost graviton region in the future. It proposes a scalar-field potential barrier at small curvature to prevent this without fine-tuning initial conditions, and sketches tests with peculiar velocities and the kSZ effect.

    Ohmic warm inflation

    Anish Ghoshal, Alessandro Strumia

    The paper argues that the warm little inflaton model has gauge anomalies, wrong thermal masses, and over-estimated friction because fermion occupations cannot follow the fast mass modulation. It proposes anomaly-free models with a ring of $N$ U(1) groups, which in the continuum limit become a fermion in a compact fifth dimension, where the inflaton is a Wilson line and friction is Ohmic dissipation that may sustain warm inflation.

    Solving fermionic sign problems with physics-informed kernels

    Kenji Fukushima, Friederike Ihssen, Renzo Kapust, Jan M. Pawlowski

    The paper extends physics-informed kernel (PIK) sampling to distributions with zeros, as typically arise in fermionic systems, by writing the target as a positively weighted sum of complex Gaussians (the PIKfan) that is then evolved with a second PIK. It demonstrates that the one-site Hubbard model and $\phi^4$-deformed variants at arbitrary chemical potential can be sampled at the cost of Gaussian sampling, with no sign problem.
