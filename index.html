  1. 1

    Scalar-tensor interactions selected through their first-order thermodynamic dynamics

    David S. Pereira

    The paper matches the effective-fluid quantities of a general scalar-tensor action (nonlinear $f(\phi,X,R)$ curvature dependence, an Einstein-tensor-Hessian coupling, and quadratic and cubic DHOST operators) to Eckart's heat-conduction and shear-viscosity laws, which sharply reduces the operator basis. Combining the resulting heat-current integrability condition with covariant degeneracy recovers the luminal Horndeski subclass, while degeneracy restricted to the scalar-unitary gauge leaves a family of nonlinear interactions that are thermodynamically compatible.

    arXiv:2611.33033gr-qchep-thquality 8

    Scalar-tensor/Horndeski gravity constrained by degeneracy conditions; modified gravity.

  2. 2

    Split Ends: UV Physics branches Black Hole Hair

    Georg Trenkler, Leonardo G. Trombetta, Alexander Vikman

    The paper UV-completes shift-symmetric scalar-Gauss-Bonnet gravity by treating the scalar as the Goldstone of a complex field with a symmetry-breaking potential. It finds static BH solutions perturbatively and numerically, showing that the modulus $\rho$ is activated for small BHs. This enhances the scalar charge and departures from GR, and for the same mass up to three branches of solutions appear with different horizon areas and surface gravities.

    arXiv:2611.31438gr-qcquality 9

    Scalar-Gauss-Bonnet black hole hair with symmetry-breaking UV completion.

  3. 3

    Romans Mass from a $\mathrm{GL}(1,\mathbb{R})$ Topological Field Theory with Boundary

    Euihun Joung, J. A. Rosabal

    The paper builds an eleven-dimensional topological field theory with a $\mathrm{GL}(1,\mathbb{R})$ connection and a boundary term whose edge modes reproduce massive type IIA supergravity. The Romans mass is a bulk zero-form $A_0$ and appears in the Chern–Simons terms. Holonomies correspond to dilaton and $(-1)^{F_L}$ monodromies fixed by D8-brane charges, and other boundary conditions give massless IIA and dual formulations.

    arXiv:2611.32437hep-thquality 9

    Topological field theory for IIA with reflection seven-branes; similar to wanted IIB paper.

  4. 4

    A cubic theory of gravity with quasitopological-like properties for compactified cosmologies

    Vicente Aguilar, Fabrizio Canfora, Vicente García, Alex Giacomini, Julio Oliva

    The paper constructs a cubic curvature invariant in $D=7$ that, on an FLRW$_4\times\Sigma_3$ ansatz, gives a first-order constraint and second-order dynamical equations. It studies de Sitter solutions with stabilized internal radius and their linear stability, shows that a class of inhomogeneous scalar perturbations has at most two time derivatives, and numerically finds that increasing the coupling delays singularity formation for $\Lambda_0>0$ and $K=-1$.

    arXiv:2611.31758hep-thgr-qcquality 8

    Higher-curvature cubic gravity with compactification, fourth-order equations, quasitopological-like.

  5. 5

    The double copy and perturbative teleparallel gravity

    Georg Stettinger

    The paper builds a minimal effective Lagrangian, up to quartic order, that reproduces the three- and four-point graviton amplitudes obtained from the gluon double copy. It fixes the Lagrangian up to a few real parameters and shows that a particular choice matches perturbative teleparallel gravity (TEGR), suggesting the teleparallel formulation makes the double copy clearer at the Lagrangian level.

    arXiv:2611.33146hep-thgr-qcquality 8🤖

    Double copy and perturbative teleparallel gravity amplitudes.

  6. 6

    Bootstrapping two-loop $\beta$-functions for bosonic and heterotic strings

    Achilleas Gitsis, Falk Hassler

    The paper obtains two-loop $\beta$-functions for the bosonic and heterotic string as a gradient flow of the low-energy effective action. The coupling-space metric is fixed by $O(d,d)$ symmetry through the Poláček-Siegel construction, with two parameters $a$ and $b$ covering both strings. It then applies the result to the two-loop flows of the integrable $\eta$- and $\lambda$-deformations, finding the heterotic correction is half the bosonic one.

    arXiv:2611.31372hep-thquality 9

    String sigma-model beta functions; marginal string theory relevance.
