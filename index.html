    Higher derivatives and ghosts through three centuries

    Andrei Smilga

    A historical and pedagogical review of higher-derivative Lagrangian systems from Euler and Ostrogradsky through Pais-Uhlenbeck. It covers benign-ghost examples in mechanics and 2D field theories, higher-derivative gravity (classically benign, quantum status open), and supersymmetric models including conformal supergravity and a 6D higher-derivative gauge theory whose ghosts are not benign.

    arXiv:2611.34097hep-thmath-phquality 9

    Review of higher-derivative ghosts, unitarity, HD gravity and conformal supergravity.

  2. 2

    Time-reflection nonlocal equations from $\mathcal{P}\mathcal{T}$-symmetric oscillator pairs: physical origin and variational formulation

    Ilmar Gahramanov, Yağ{\i}zhan Şahin

    The paper shows that second-order equations with time reflection, such as $\ddot{x}=\lambda[x(t)-x(-t)]+\mu[\dot{x}(t)-\dot{x}(-t)]$, describe the $\mathcal{P}\mathcal{T}$-symmetric solutions of a local pair of oscillators with balanced gain/loss and gyroscopic coupling. It analyzes Lagrangians (none regular first-order when $\lambda\mu\neq0$), exceptional points and Jordan blocks, and constructs a Bateman-type Hamiltonian where $\mu$ is removed by a canonical transformation.

    arXiv:2611.33186math-phhep-thcross-listquality 8

    PT-symmetric oscillator pairs, higher-derivative Lagrangian, Bateman Hamiltonian.

  3. 3

    An Argument for the Weak Gravity Conjecture from Constrained Wormholes

    Gabriele Di Ubaldo, Luca V. Iliesiu, Dawid Maskalaniec

    The paper constructs constrained-instanton two- and multi-boundary wormholes in Einstein-Maxwell theory and shows that, without a charged particle obeying the WGC, the resulting amplitudes violate positivity of the gravitational path integral, because $\kappa_2$ grows too fast beyond the extremal potential $\Phi_{\rm ext}$. Requiring a charged-particle instability to restore positivity yields the electric, magnetic and dyonic WGC and the convex hull condition.

    arXiv:2611.33415hep-thquality 9

    Weak gravity conjecture argued from gravitational path integral positivity; swampland-adjacent.

  4. 4

    An algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone

    Venkatesa Chandrasekaran

    The paper proposes a background-independent quantization of 4D vacuum GR by solving the constraints on null cones of a causal diamond, quantizing the reduced phase space, and building a Hilbert space with a type I algebra. From the resulting Lorentzian path integral it derives a finite-dimensional Hilbert space for bounded diamonds, the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, the one-loop logarithmic correction matching Sen's coefficient, and the semiclassical Einstein equations.

    arXiv:2611.34924hep-thgr-qcmath-phquality 8

    Nonperturbative quantization of gravity, finite Hilbert space, one-loop corrections.

  5. 5

    The String Theory Photinoverse

    Andreas P. Braun, Thibaut Coudarchet, Arthur Hebecker, Joerg Jaeckel, Jonathan Steiner

    The paper studies RR-photinos, the superpartners of RR-photons in type-IIB orientifolds. Wilson-line-induced kinetic mixing, together with SUSY-breaking photino masses, makes them mix with the bino. The authors compute the photino masses and couplings in LVS as functions of the volume $\mathcal{V}$, derive relic abundances from volume-modulus and inflaton decays, and find $\gamma$-ray signals that could probe dark matter.

    arXiv:2611.33273hep-thquality 9

    IIB compactification dark photinos; phenomenology-focused string paper.

  6. 6

    Machine Learning for Calabi--Yau Geometry and Heterotic Compactification

    Vishnu Jejjala, Damian Mayorga Pena

    A review of machine learning methods for Calabi-Yau geometry: predicting topological invariants, searching for new Calabi-Yau fourfolds, and numerically approximating Ricci-flat metrics. It also covers how bundle metrics and harmonic forms enter canonically normalized Yukawa couplings in heterotic compactifications, plus datasets, software and error control.

    arXiv:2611.33311hep-thhep-phquality 9

    ML review for Calabi-Yau heterotic compactification; not M/F-theory.

  7. 7

    All known supersymmetric $\mathrm{AdS}_4 \times S^7$ uplifts from maximal supergravity

    Krzysztof Pilch, Oscar Varela

    The paper uplifts the seven-chiral $\mathcal{N}=1$ sector of SO(8)-gauged $D=4$ $\mathcal{N}=8$ supergravity to eleven dimensions, giving a unified description of all known supersymmetric AdS$_4$ vacua. It then obtains new explicit $D=11$ uplifts of the U(1)$^2$ and SO(3) vacua and describes their internal geometries as abelian and non-abelian Kaluza–Klein forms.

    arXiv:2611.33564hep-thgr-qcquality 9

    Supergravity AdS4 uplifts to 11D; string compactification-adjacent.

  8. 8

    Resolving the Conformal Mode Problem with Einstein-Cartan Gravity

    Stephon Alexander, Raymond Isichei, João Magueijo

    The paper observes that in first-order Euclidean Einstein-Cartan gravity with an independent spin connection, a conformal rescaling of the tetrad produces no kinetic term for $\phi$, so the action is bounded below for $\Lambda>0$. It shows how the usual negative kinetic term returns once the connection is set to its Levi-Civita value, and how Einstein-Hilbert and $R^2/\Lambda$ gravity arise as saddles.

    arXiv:2611.33278gr-qchep-thquality 7

    Conformal mode problem, Einstein-Cartan, R^2 gravity as saddle.

  9. 9

    Lower Bound on the Lightest Neutrino Mass with Resonant Neutrino Self-Interactions

    Shao-Feng Ge, Joao Paulo Pinheiro, Shaoyang Qin, Ruitao Sun, Oleg Titov

    The authors show that resonant $s$-channel scattering of UHE astrophysical neutrinos on the cosmic neutrino background via an MeV scalar mediator gives a dip whose edges depend on the lightest neutrino mass. They solve coupled neutrino/antineutrino transport equations with double regeneration and fit IceCube HESE 7.5-year data. This gives a lower bound $m_{\rm lightest}\gtrsim 7.8\times10^{-3}$ eV at $m_\phi=1$ MeV, which combined with DESI confines the lightest mass to about $10^{-2}$ eV.

    arXiv:2611.34213hep-phastro-ph.HEhep-exquality 8

    Neutrino phenomenology, out of scope.

  10. 10

    Lighting up LZ with Loopy Transitions in a leptophilic Dark Sector

    Simone Biondini, Clara Hofheinz, Stefan Melchin, Lorenzo Tiberi, Stefan Vogl

    The paper studies a leptophilic scalar-vector dark sector coupled to right-handed leptons through a vector-like charged mediator, with a mass splitting of a few hundred keV. It shows that the loop-induced inelastic scalar-vector photon coupling arises at dimension five while elastic scattering arises only at dimension six, and that endothermic scattering can account for the single high-energy LZ nuclear recoil at $E_R\approx 248$ keV with thermal relic abundance.

    arXiv:2611.34438hep-phquality 8

    Dark matter phenomenology.

  11. 11

    Ultralight Dark Matter Spectrum with Gravitational Focusing and Doppler Motion

    Shao-Feng Ge, Yong Tang, Yue-Hui Yao

    The paper computes how solar gravitational focusing, via the Coulomb-wave factor $\chi_{\mathbf{k}}$, and the detector's Doppler motion modify the ultralight dark matter signal spectrum. It finds percent-level corrections with an annual modulation, and shows with Bayesian inference on simulated data that neglecting them biases the recovered mass and DM wind velocity once the observation time exceeds roughly $10^2$ coherence times.

    arXiv:2611.34464hep-phgr-qcquality 8

    Ultralight dark matter detection phenomenology.
