Dr. Scout

A daily digest of new arXiv papers in quantum gravity and nearby fields.

Dr. Scout is a small, private email digest. Every weekday it picks out the new arXiv papers that match one researcher's interests and sends them to a few colleagues.

How it works

Each weekday morning a script reads the new submissions and cross-lists in hep-th, gr-qc, hep-ph and astro-ph.CO. Claude, a language model made by Anthropic, reads every title and abstract and rates how well the paper fits the topics listed below.

The best matches then go through a quality check. Papers by authors in the curator's research network get this check too, whatever their topic. Claude reads the beginning and end of each paper and looks at its authors' publication records on INSPIRE-HEP. It looks for papers whose claims go well beyond what they actually work out, including papers that seem to be mostly machine-generated text with little physics behind it. A short publication record alone does not count against anyone.

Papers that pass are ranked by relevance and quality, with a small boost for authors in the curator's network, and at most 30 go out each day. Each entry comes with a short summary of the paper and both scores.

The screening is automated and makes mistakes in both directions. If a paper is missing from the digest, that says very little about the paper.

Main topics

  1. Quadratic / higher-derivative gravity (Stelle gravity, R² + Weyl² actions, fourth-order theories)
  2. Weyl (conformal) gravity
  3. Ghost problem in QFT and gravity: massive spin-2 ghosts, Lee–Wick theories, Ostrogradsky instability
  4. Unitarity of quantum gravity theories
  5. PT-symmetric and pseudo-Hermitian (non-Hermitian) quantum mechanics and QFT
  6. Covariant operator quantization, BRST, Kugo–Ojima quartet mechanism, indefinite-metric state spaces
  7. Classical scale invariance, spontaneous breaking of scale/conformal symmetry, dimensional transmutation (Coleman–Weinberg, Gildener–Weinberg)
  8. Dynamical generation of the Planck scale and electroweak scale ("agravity"-type models)
  9. Inflation in modified/quadratic gravity (Starobinsky-type, scale-invariant inflation), inflationary observables vs. Planck/BICEP
  10. Primordial gravitational waves, tensor power spectrum, tensor-to-scalar ratio
  11. Swampland program, cobordism conjecture, R7-branes and other non-supersymmetric branes
  12. Codimension-two defects/vortices (Alice strings, cosmic strings) in gravity and supergravity; axio-dilaton gravity; F-theory / IIB backgrounds with SL(2,Z) monodromy
  13. Braneworlds and extra dimensions (warped compactifications, Randall–Sundrum-type models, bulk horizons)
  14. Hierarchy problems: Planck/electroweak hierarchy, cosmological constant problem

Related topics

  1. Asymptotic safety and other UV completions of gravity (incl. functional RG for gravity)
  2. Non-local / infinite-derivative gravity, ghost-free higher-derivative theories
  3. Effective field theory of gravity; positivity, causality and unitarity bounds on higher-derivative terms
  4. Graviton scattering amplitudes and perturbative quantum gravity
  5. Massive gravity, bimetric gravity, spin-2 field theory (Fierz–Pauli)
  6. Black holes and other exact solutions in higher-derivative / modified gravity
  7. Scalar–tensor theories, Higgs inflation, non-minimal couplings
  8. Trace/Weyl anomalies, conformal field theory coupled to gravity, Weyl geometry
  9. Constrained Hamiltonian systems, BV formalism, gauge-fixing and quantization of gauge theories
  10. Generalized/higher-form symmetries, solitons, monopoles, topological defects
  11. String compactifications, string phenomenology, non-supersymmetric string theory, other swampland conjectures (WGC, distance, de Sitter)
  12. Early-universe cosmology beyond inflation (reheating, phase transitions, stochastic GW backgrounds)
  13. Classically scale-invariant extensions of the Standard Model; neutrino masses via seesaw / "neutrino option"; dark matter in such models

Left out unless they touch the topics above: experimental/observational papers, lattice QCD, collider phenomenology, condensed matter, quantum information.
