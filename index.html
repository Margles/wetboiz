1. Resummations In Higher-Curvature Modifications Of Starobinsky Inflation
Marco Piva · 2611.13112 [hep-th, gr-qc]
Shows that the singular dependence of Starobinsky-inflation observables on higher-curvature couplings can be resummed in the ratio gamma/alpha, giving an improved slow-roll expansion that reduces smoothly to Starobinsky as gamma goes to 0. Computes next-to-leading-order observables for an R^3 term, finds that they differ from first-order results only outside the data-favored n_s range, and comments on stability in renormalizable (fakeon) quantum gravity.
relevance 9 · quality 7 · Higher-curvature corrections to Starobinsky inflation, power spectra and r.
2. Moduli spaces in CFT: vortex defects
Gabriel Cuomo, Adar Sharon · 2611.13092 [hep-th]
The paper constructs codimension-two conformal vortex defects in CFTs with moduli spaces and a broken continuous symmetry, as saddle points of the moduli-space EFT, generalizing Gukov-Witten and ABJM vortex loops without supersymmetry. It reproduces localization results for Gukov-Witten operators, computes the g-function for 1/2-BPS vortex loops in ABJM, and relates defect spectra to massive particles in the broken vacuum.
relevance 5 · quality 8 · Codimension-two conformal vortex defects with broken conformal symmetry.
3. The Higgs boson, naturalness, and the Universe
Mikhail Shaposhnikov · 2611.14370 [hep-ph, hep-th]
Review of the Higgs boson's role in particle physics and cosmology within the SM and the nuMSM. It covers vacuum metastability, naturalness, Higgs inflation, reheating, the electroweak phase transition and baryogenesis.
relevance 5 · quality 8 · Review covering the Higgs naturalness/hierarchy problem and Higgs inflation.
4. Cosmic String Networks with Time-Varying Tension: Field-Theory Simulations and an Analytic Model
José Correia, Ivan Rybak · 2611.14086 [astro-ph.CO, hep-ph]
The authors run the first field-theory simulations of Abelian-Higgs cosmic string networks with tension varying as a power of the scale factor. Networks reach the expected scaling regime except when field oscillations redshift more slowly than the potential, and the results agree with a calibrated velocity-dependent one-scale model.
relevance 6 · quality 8 · Cosmic string network simulations; topological defects, GW-relevant but no GW computation.
5. Stellar Scalar Monopoles from Gauss--Bonnet Curvature beyond Four Dimensions
Sudan Hansraj · 2611.14212 [gr-qc]
For static stars above four dimensions in scalar-Gauss-Bonnet gravity at first order in the coupling, the scalar charge is an interior integral weighted by energy density plus radial pressure, so it is nonzero and has the sign of the coupling, opposite to a Schwarzschild-Tangherlini black hole. The four-dimensional absence of stellar charge follows because the spatial Gauss-Bonnet density vanishes in three dimensions.
relevance 6 · quality 7 · Scalar-Gauss-Bonnet stellar scalar charge in higher dimensions.
6. Four-charge Rotating AdS Black Holes in Gauged STU Supergravity
Liang Ma, H. Lu · 2611.13074 [hep-th, gr-qc]
Constructs the general non-extremal rotating AdS4 black hole with four independent charges in gauged STU supergravity and derives its thermodynamics. The BPS limit reproduces the known entropy matching the dual CFT, and the solution is shown to be free of closed timelike curves outside the horizon.
relevance 3 · quality 8 · Exact rotating AdS black holes in gauged supergravity; no listed topic.
7. Dynamical obstructions to primary Proca hair and its logarithmic Maxwell continuation
Anirudh Pradhan, K. Ghaderi, Ulug'bek Eshkarayev, Sardor Sabirov, Javlon Rayimbaev · 2611.14344 [gr-qc]
The paper studies a regularized generalized Proca theory with Gauss-Bonnet gravity and shows the constant-norm hairy black holes cannot have both a positive dipole kinetic term and real radial tensor characteristics. Adding a Maxwell term gives a logarithmic-norm branch with regular horizons and finite mass, but a longitudinal even angular mode with negative squared speed remains.
relevance 7 · quality 5 · Vector-Gauss-Bonnet hair, Hamiltonian analysis, ghost-like angular instability.
8. Yang-Lee edge singularity at $\mathcal{O}(\partial^4)$ from the functional renormalization group: revised analysis
Konrad Kockler, Jan M. Pawlowski, Vladimir V. Skokov · 2611.14206 [hep-th]
Determines Yang-Lee edge singularity exponents as functions of dimension using the functional renormalization group to fourth order in the derivative expansion, matching epsilon-expansion and exact 2d values. Attributes earlier hyperscaling violations to ignoring the motion of the expansion point.
relevance 1 · quality 8 · Yang-Lee functional RG; not gravity.
