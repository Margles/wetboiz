wetboiz Daily Digest

A daily digest of new arXiv papers in quantum gravity and nearby fields.

Each weekday morning a script reads the new submissions and cross-lists in hep-th, gr-qc, hep-ph and astro-ph.CO. Claude, a language model made by Anthropic, reads every title and abstract and rates how well the paper fits the topics listed below.

The best matches then go through a quality check. Papers by authors in the curator's research network get this check too, whatever their topic. Claude reads the beginning and end of each paper and looks at its authors' publication records on INSPIRE-HEP. It looks for papers whose claims go well beyond what they actually work out, including papers that seem to be mostly machine-generated text with little physics behind it. A short publication record alone does not count against anyone.

Papers that pass are ranked by relevance and quality, with a small boost for authors in the curator's network, and at most 30 go out each day. Each entry comes with a short summary of the paper and both scores.

The screening is automated and makes mistakes in both directions. If a paper is missing from the digest, that says very little about the paper.

Main topics

  • Classification of 5D SCFTs; M-theory on Calabi–Yau threefolds with canonical singularities, geometric engineering, shrinkable surfaces and their gluings
  • 6D SCFTs and F-theory on elliptically fibered Calabi–Yau threefolds; circle reductions, Kaluza–Klein theories, descent from 6D to 5D
  • 5D gauge theories, (p,q) 5-brane webs and 7-branes, toric and non-toric Calabi–Yau geometry, prepotentials and Coulomb branches
  • Swampland program, cobordism conjecture, R7-branes and other non-supersymmetric branes
  • Codimension-two defects/vortices (Alice strings, cosmic strings) in gravity and supergravity; axio-dilaton gravity; F-theory / IIB backgrounds with SL(2,Z) monodromy
  • Quadratic / higher-derivative gravity (Stelle gravity, R² + Weyl² actions, fourth-order theories)
  • Ghost problem in QFT and gravity: massive spin-2 ghosts, Lee–Wick theories, Ostrogradsky instability
  • Unitarity of quantum gravity theories
  • PT-symmetric and pseudo-Hermitian (non-Hermitian) quantum mechanics and QFT
  • Covariant operator quantization, BRST, Kugo–Ojima quartet mechanism, indefinite-metric state spaces
  • Primordial gravitational waves, tensor power spectrum, tensor-to-scalar ratio
  • Inflation in modified/quadratic gravity (Starobinsky-type, scale-invariant inflation), inflationary observables vs. Planck/BICEP
  • Weyl (conformal) gravity
  • Classical scale invariance, spontaneous breaking of scale/conformal symmetry, dimensional transmutation (Coleman–Weinberg, Gildener–Weinberg)
  • Dynamical generation of the Planck scale and electroweak scale ("agravity"-type models)
  • Braneworlds and extra dimensions (warped compactifications, Randall–Sundrum-type models, bulk horizons)
  • Hierarchy problems: Planck/electroweak hierarchy, cosmological constant problem

Related topics

  • Generalized/higher-form symmetries, solitons, monopoles, topological defects
  • String compactifications, string phenomenology, non-supersymmetric string theory, other swampland conjectures (WGC, distance, de Sitter)
  • Machine learning, AI agents and formal verification (e.g. Lean) applied to theoretical physics and geometry
  • Quantum information in physics: entanglement in QFT and gravity, holographic entanglement, quantum error correction, quantum computing
  • Asymptotic safety and other UV completions of gravity (incl. functional RG for gravity)
  • Non-local / infinite-derivative gravity, ghost-free higher-derivative theories
  • Effective field theory of gravity; positivity, causality and unitarity bounds on higher-derivative terms
  • Graviton scattering amplitudes and perturbative quantum gravity
  • Massive gravity, bimetric gravity, spin-2 field theory (Fierz–Pauli)
  • Black holes and other exact solutions in higher-derivative / modified gravity
  • Trace/Weyl anomalies, conformal field theory coupled to gravity, Weyl geometry
  • Constrained Hamiltonian systems, BV formalism, gauge-fixing and quantization of gauge theories
  • Scalar–tensor theories, Higgs inflation, non-minimal couplings
  • Early-universe cosmology beyond inflation (reheating, phase transitions, stochastic GW backgrounds)
  • Classically scale-invariant extensions of the Standard Model; neutrino masses via seesaw / "neutrino option"; dark matter in such models

Left out unless they touch the topics above: experimental/observational papers, lattice QCD, collider phenomenology, condensed matter.
