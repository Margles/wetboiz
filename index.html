    Rotating black hole thermodynamics at NNLO in higher-curvature gravity

    Cristobal Laporte

    Using the Reall–Santos Euclidean-action method extended to NNLO, the paper computes the Gibbs free energy of five-dimensional asymptotically flat equal-spin rotating black holes in Einstein–Gauss–Bonnet plus cubic quasitopological gravity, with full spin dependence. It derives the corrected mass, entropy and angular momenta, and shows that the corrections change sign with spin and contain logarithms of the spin parameter, with non-uniform behaviour near extremality.

    arXiv:2611.24874hep-thgr-qcquality 8

    Black hole thermodynamics in quasitopological and Gauss-Bonnet higher-curvature gravity.

  2. 2

    A relativistic gravitisation of quantum theory?

    Samuel Fedida, Adrian Kent

    The paper proposes 'local semiclassical gravity': semiclassical Einstein equations sourced by the local quantum state, updated by collapses in the past light cone, which gives a causal completion of the Schrödinger-Newton equation in the nonrelativistic limit. It shows that no superluminal signalling occurs, while the theory violates quantum no-signalling, allows approximate state cloning via nonlinear readout devices, and is not locally causal in the Bell sense. It also gives weak-field examples and discusses tabletop tests.

    arXiv:2611.24227gr-qcmath-phquant-phquality 9

    Semiclassical gravity coupled to quantum states; tests of quantum nature of gravity.

  3. 3

    A Positive Metric on Four-Dimensional QFTs

    Thomas Hartman, Grégoire Mathys, Andreas Stergiou

    The paper defines a non-perturbative generalization of the Jack-Osborn metric from the spectral function of $\langle \mathcal{O}_I \mathcal{E} \mathcal{O}_J\rangle$ in impact-parameter space. The ANEC makes this metric positive, giving a monotonic $a$-function and hence the strong $a$-theorem. It also shows that a scheme exists in the perturbative local RG where the Jack-Osborn metric matches this spectral function.

    arXiv:2611.23370hep-thquality 10

    a-theorem and ANEC in QFT; not a listed topic.

    Only the disk: topological obstructions for warped AdS$_6\times S^2$ in Type IIB

    Nakwoo Kim

    The paper proves that regular supersymmetric warped $\mathrm{AdS}_6\times S^2$ Type IIB solutions without seven-branes must have a disk as the Riemann surface $\Sigma$. It combines a bound on the boundary phase winding of $d\mathcal{A}_+/d\mathcal{A}_-$ with the zero count on the Schottky double, extends the result to mutually local seven-brane and orientifold punctures under stated assumptions, and recovers the known ordering of the five-brane charges.

    arXiv:2611.24320hep-thquality 8🤖

    AdS6 Type IIB solutions with five-branes and seven-branes; 5d SCFT duals.

  5. 5

    Melvin-type Electromagnetized Kerr Black Hole in Kaluza-Klein and Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton-axion Theories

    Liang Ma, Zhi-Chao Li, H. Lu

    The paper modifies the five-to-four (uplift, twist, reduce) and four-to-three $SL(3,\mathbb{R})$ solution-generating procedures in Kaluza-Klein theory so that a Schwarzschild or Kerr black hole can be immersed in both electric and magnetic Melvin-type fields without a Taub-swirling term. It also gives the analogous Kerr solution in Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton-axion theory and rederives the Gibbons-Wiltshire and Rasheed-Larsen solutions via the modified route.

    arXiv:2611.23379hep-thgr-qcquality 8

    Melvin Kerr solutions in KK and EMDA theory; Kaluza-Klein solution generation.

  6. 6

    Non-Perturbative Moduli Stabilization with Sp$(4,\mathbb Z)$ Modular Symmetry

    Miguel Hernandez-Segura, Xiang-Gan Liu, Hans Peter Nilles, Saul Ramos-Sanchez

    The paper constructs the general pole-restricted Sp$(4,\mathbb Z)$-covariant gaugino-condensate superpotentials for heterotic threshold classes, using basic modular-invariant ratios and their syzygies. It finds continuous surfaces and curves of supersymmetric Minkowski vacua, shows that only the $S_4$ and $D_{12}$ fixed points stabilize all three moduli, and also constructs isolated vacua away from fixed points, including some near the moduli values preferred by genus-two flavor fits.

    arXiv:2611.23381hep-thquality 8

    Moduli stabilization with modular symmetry in heterotic supergravity.

    An Atiyah--Hirzebruch to Adams map and the hastened Adams spectral sequence for supercohomology

    Arun Debray, Weicheng Ye, Matthew Yu

    The paper constructs a natural map of filtered spectra from the Atiyah–Hirzebruch filtration to the Adams filtration for $\mathit{ko}$- and $\ell$-modules, with a regrading of the filtration. It uses this map to build a hastened Adams spectral sequence for $\tau_{\le n}\mathit{ko}$ and applies it to supercohomology, including worked examples such as $B\mathbb{Z}/2n$, $K(\mathbb{Z}/2,2)$ and $BQ_8$, along with a user's guide and charts for common $\mathcal{A}(1)$-modules.

    arXiv:2611.24725math.ATcond-mat.str-elhep-thmath-phcross-listquality 10

    Math spectral sequences for supercohomology; peripheral to interests.

    Localization of a 3-form gauge field in a braneworld

    Pratik Chattopadhyay, M. J. Neves

    The paper studies localization of a 3-form gauge field on a 5D warped AdS thick brane with a background scalar. It then couples the dual field strength to a complex scalar with coupling $g_0$, finds an induced effective Kalb-Ramond kinetic term, and argues from numerical plots that resonance peaks signal its localization.

    arXiv:2611.24510hep-thquality 7

    Localization of p-form gauge field in warped AdS braneworld.
