    Coupling of TQFTs to QFTs and SymTFT interfaces from bordisms

    Max Hübner, Ling Lin, Daniel Robbins, Subham Roy

    The paper constructs SymTFT topological interfaces from geometric bordisms in M-theory local models with torsional cycles. For the circle compactification of a frozen $D_5$ singularity, it finds an interface between the $\mathbb{Z}_4$ and $\mathbb{Z}_2$ SymTFTs, and shows that the coupling of the $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ gauge sector to an extra TQFT explains the electric-magnetic symmetry mismatch.

    arXiv:2611.25012hep-thquality 9

    M-theory singularities, SymTFT, frozen singularity; adjacent to geometric engineering.

    UV Bounds on Inflationary Parity Violation

    Alexander Cassem, Mark P. Hertzberg

    The paper shows that in UV completions of the dynamical Chern-Simons operator (chiral fermions, heterotic and type IIB string compactifications), a consistent EFT forces the chirality parameter $|\Pi_c|\ll 1$. Together with existing tensor-scalar-scalar bispectrum bounds, this means a detectable parity-odd scalar trispectrum from inflation would need an extreme enhancement of the TSS vertex.

    arXiv:2611.26594hep-thastro-ph.COgr-qchep-phquality 8🤖

    Parity violation in inflation from Chern-Simons EFT, UV completion bounds, tensor-to-scalar ratio.

  3. 3

    Actionless Branes on Axion Wormholes

    A. Ramesh Chandra

    The paper shows that on the two-axion type IIB Calabi–Yau Euclidean wormhole, a fivebrane carrying string charge (a D5 with flux or a D5–NS5 bound state in S-dual variables) becomes actionless before the wormhole reaches infinity, at $\tau_*/\tau_{\rm end}<2/3$. It extends this to all Calabi–Yau threefolds along Kähler-cone rays and proves a convex-hull bound for the Kähler axions from the cubic volume. The result realises the extra instantons required by the imaginary distance bound and the convex-hull axion weak gravity conjecture.

    arXiv:2611.25026hep-thgr-qcquality 9🤖

    Axion wormholes, weak gravity conjecture, branes in IIB; swampland-related.

    Upper Bounds on the Radion Mass in a Warped Extra Dimension

    Leandro Da Rold, Eugenio Megias, Mariano Quiros, Lindber Salas

    The paper derives upper bounds on the radion-to-first-graviton mass ratio in 5D warped models with Goldberger-Wise stabilization and naked IR singularities, finding $R_{11}<1$ for exponential models as $\nu\to1^+$ (the linear dilaton limit) and $R_{21}\lesssim 1.374$ at $\nu=2$. It also checks that the full radion tower satisfies the $h_1h_1\to h_1h_1$ unitarity sum rule, with the scalar contribution $S_\infty\simeq-0.30<0$.

    arXiv:2611.25473hep-thhep-phquality 9

    Radion mass bounds in warped extra dimension; unitarity sum rules.

    Worldsheet Effective Theory of Abelian-Higgs Cosmic Strings beyond Nambu--Goto

    Sergio Alameda-Calvo, Josu C. Aurrekoetxea, Jose J. Blanco-Pillado, Alberto García Martín-Caro, J. M. Queiruga

    The paper derives a covariant worldsheet effective action for Abelian-Higgs strings at critical coupling, extending Nambu-Goto with a massive scalar shape mode and a massive vector mode, whose coefficients are fixed by transverse integrals over the vortex profile. The shape mode couples to intrinsic worldsheet curvature and the dual pseudoscalar to normal-bundle curvature, and the resulting predictions for parametric instabilities and for mode excitation in colliding transverse waves are compared with adaptive-mesh-refinement lattice simulations.

    arXiv:2611.26013hep-thastro-ph.COhep-phquality 9

    Cosmic string worldsheet effective theory beyond Nambu-Goto; topological defects.

  6. 6

    2-dimensional Metric-Affine Geometries and Dynamical Gravity

    Damianos Iosifidis

    Shows that in 2D metric-affine geometry the full curvature tensor is determined by the (asymmetric) Ricci tensor, so non-metric spaces are not Einstein spaces, and finds an enlarged symmetry of the scalar curvature under vectorial connection shifts. Applies this to quadratic metric-affine gravity in 2D, obtaining Klein-Gordon-like equations for $R$ that give torsion and non-metricity nontrivial dynamics.

    arXiv:2611.24945gr-qchep-thmath.DGquality 7

    Metric-affine geometry and gravity theories in 2D; touches modified gravity directly.

  7. 7

    Log-periodic radiation cores and light rings in pure quadratic bulk gravity

    Sudan Hansraj, Rio Bissessur

    In the pure quadratic $f(G)$ bulk gravity model, the paper shows that regular-centre static solutions carry no matter, so it studies singular cores via an autonomous system in scale-invariant variables. For radiation ($p=\rho/3$) the system is integrable with a nonlinear centre and log-periodic solutions, which give alternating stable and unstable light rings at geometrically spaced radii with ratio about 2.165, visible as a periodic tube in the optical geometry.

    arXiv:2611.26138gr-qcquality 7

    Pure quadratic bulk gravity, scale-invariant, star cores and light rings.

  8. 8

    Cardinal RG=GR: Phase-Space Cardinality and the Cosmological Constant and Hierarchy Problems

    Laurent Freidel, Jerzy Kowalski-Glikman, Robert G. Leigh, Djordje Minic

    The paper restricts the quantum theory to a finite phase-space region of dimensionless cardinality $\mathcal{N}$, which ties the spacetime and momentum scales together. On Euclidean de Sitter space this makes geometric stationarity equivalent to stationarity in the momentum scale ("Cardinal RG=GR"). It relates $\mathcal{N}$ to the Gibbons-Hawking entropy, obtains $\Lambda\propto 1/\sqrt{\mathcal{N}}$, argues that $\Lambda$ and a scalar mass parameter are radiatively stable, and recovers conventional QFT in a double-scaling limit $G\to0$, $\Lambda\to0$.

    arXiv:2611.26170hep-thgr-qchep-phquality 8

    Cosmological constant and hierarchy problem via phase-space cardinality.

  9. 9

    Bianchi cosmologies with four-dimensional groups of motion in New General Relativity

    Manuel Hohmann, Paul Martin Kull, Margus Saal

    The paper derives the most general teleparallel tetrads and compatible anisotropic-fluid energy-momentum tensors for Bianchi types II, III and IX with four-dimensional symmetry groups, reducing each to four free functions of time. It then writes the 1-parameter New General Relativity field equations as a dynamical system and finds particular solutions, including a branch reproducing the closed-universe Friedmann equations with $\Lambda=0$ and projective fixed points whose existence depends on the parameter $\delta$.

    arXiv:2611.26474gr-qchep-thquality 8

    Teleparallel New General Relativity Bianchi cosmologies; modified gravity solutions.

    Asymmetric Orbifolds and 4-dimensional $\mathcal N=4$ String Islands

    Gerardo Aldazabal, Eduardo C. Andrés, Anamaría Font, Elisa I. Marieni, Kumar Narain, Ida G. Zadeh

    The paper builds heterotic and type II $\mathbb{T}^6/\mathbb{Z}_n$ asymmetric orbifolds with 16 supersymmetries and no massless moduli other than the dilaton, giving 4d string islands. It classifies 28 heterotic island candidates from Leech lattice automorphisms and the allowed type II $\Gamma(6,6)$ lattices and orbifold generators, proves full consistency for several models, and examines archipelagos of inequivalent realizations.

    arXiv:2611.25020hep-thquality 9

    String islands via asymmetric orbifolds; string compactification, no swampland focus.

    The BSM Rollercoaster, 1980-2000

    Savas Dimopoulos

    A participant's historical account of BSM physics from 1978 to the early 2000s, tracing waves of optimism and disappointment around technicolor, supersymmetry, grand unification, large extra dimensions, and the anthropic cosmological constant, leading to Split Supersymmetry. It also reflects on how the status of naturalness changed.

    arXiv:2611.25060hep-phhep-exhep-thphysics.hist-phquality 9

    Historical review touching naturalness and extra dimensions.

    Revisiting Kerr-Newman-(A)dS Black Holes in \(f(R)\) Gravity: A Kerr-Schild Approach

    Göksel Daylan Esmer, Saliha Türkmen

    The paper re-derives the Kerr-Newman-(A)dS solutions in constant-curvature $f(R)$ gravity with a generalized Kerr-Schild ansatz, solving the Maxwell equations independently of the Kerr-Schild function. For quadratic $f(R)$ gravity it shows that the field equations within this ansatz force $R$ to be constant, so charged rotating solutions reduce to Kerr-Newman-(A)dS with a rescaled charge.

    arXiv:2611.25992gr-qcquality 7

    Kerr-Newman-(A)dS black holes in f(R) and quadratic gravity.

    Fast extraction of universal data from temporal spectrum: Applications to Haagerup CFT and monopole scattering

    Tokiro Numasawa, Boris De Vos, Masataka Watanabe, Atsushi Ueda

    The paper extracts BCFT spectra from the transfer matrix of imaginary-time-evolved boundary iMPS, using open-closed duality, and benchmarks it on the Ising and Potts chains. It then obtains the Haagerup CFT charge-resolved spectrum to higher levels with $c=2.137(27)$ and realizes the Maldacena-Ludwig boundary from the Fidkowski-Kitaev ground state, with spectra matching BCFT predictions.

    arXiv:2611.26257cond-mat.str-elhep-lathep-thquant-phcross-listquality 9

    Lattice CFT spectrum extraction, monopole boundary; weak overlap with monopoles.

    The Clifford Hierarchy from Clifford Stabilizers in 2D

    Alison Warman, Sakura Schafer-Nameki

    The paper constructs 2D topological qubit codes from quantum doubles of $D_4^{k-1}\times\mathbb{Z}_2$ (and a product of two such groups) with Clifford stabilizers and a constant-depth logical $C^{k-1}Z$ gate at level $k$ of the Clifford hierarchy. The gate is a layer of physical $Z$ or $CZ$ gates, which refutes the conjectured bound of one level above the stabilizers. It also proves with a rank argument that the gate's level cannot be lowered by a change of logical basis.

    arXiv:2611.25025quant-phcond-mat.str-elhep-thmath.QAcross-listquality 8🤖

    Quantum error-correcting codes and Clifford hierarchy; only loosely quantum information.

    Subsolar neutron star-black hole binaries from collapsars: Gravitational waveform models and detectability in current searches

    Vishal Baibhav, Valerio De Luca, Loris Del Grosso, Emanuele Berti, Brian D. Metzger, Lam Hui

    The paper builds a frequency-domain waveform model, NSBHc, for subsolar neutron star-black hole mergers formed in fragmenting collapsar disks, with tidal disruption, large finite-size effects, eccentricity and gas-driven dephasing. Fitting factors against the LVK O4b template bank show that current subsolar searches miss up to $30\%$ of events for $q>0.1$ and more than $90\%$ for $m_{\rm NS}\sim 0.1\,M_\odot$, which weakens merger-rate limits on this channel.

    arXiv:2611.25040gr-qcastro-ph.COastro-ph.HEquality 9

    Astrophysical GW waveform detectability; observational.

    Dark Matter Model Implications of a Candidate Sub-halo Near the Sun, and the Data Needed to Discriminate Among Them

    Stefano Profumo, Sukanya Chakrabarti

    The paper assumes the pulsar-timing-acceleration sub-halo candidate near the Sun (mass of a few $10^7 M_\odot$) is real and works out what it would imply for dark matter models, including ultralight, warm, self-interacting, decaying and primordial black hole dark matter. It re-examines the statistics of the original detection and proposes four measurements (interior accelerations, lensing and accretion, emission morphology, comoving stars) that could discriminate among the models.

    arXiv:2611.25359astro-ph.GAastro-ph.COastro-ph.HEhep-phcross-listquality 9

    Dark matter sub-halo implications; out of scope.

  17. 17

    Majorana dark matter from a thick-to-thin brane junction: Modal stability, real-mediator coannihilation, and a dark-radiation imprint

    F. A. Brito

    The paper builds a thermal Majorana dark-matter candidate from the lightest massive localized fermion of a $D=7$ thick-to-thin brane junction. A modal selection rule makes the lighter state stable, and a real scalar mediator gives $s$-wave annihilation. It computes the relic coupling, with $\Omega_X h^2=0.120$ at $g\simeq0.17$ for $M_-\simeq 42$ GeV, and predicts $0.05\lesssim\Delta N_{\rm eff}\lesssim0.076$ from the light chiral mode.

    arXiv:2611.25872hep-phhep-thquality 7

    Brane junction dark matter model; braneworld flavor but phenomenology focused.

    Dirac leptogenesis with charged leptons

    Juan P. Garcés, Florian Goertz, Maya Hager

    The paper extends Dirac leptogenesis from right-handed neutrinos to SM charged leptons by keeping the Yukawa interactions as higher-dimensional Froggatt-Nielsen operators until after sphaleron freeze-out, which delays left-right equilibration. Requiring a supercooled flavon phase transition, with reheating below sphaleron temperatures and lepton-flavor-violation bounds, it finds a viable flavon mass of roughly $1.7$-$50$ GeV.

    arXiv:2611.25029hep-phastro-ph.COquality 8

    Leptogenesis phenomenology with supercooled phase transition; out of scope.

    Direct simulation Monte Carlo for dark matter production and phase-space evolution

    Felix Kahlhoefer, Ann Cathrin Wesener

    The authors adapt the Direct Simulation Monte Carlo method from rarefied gas dynamics to early-universe dark matter production. They implement freeze-out, decays, elastic $2\leftrightarrow 2$ scattering and number-changing $2\leftrightarrow 3$ processes without a momentum grid, validate them against other Boltzmann solvers, and apply them to a two-step production scenario with a bimodal phase-space distribution.

    arXiv:2611.25812hep-phastro-ph.COquality 9

    Refractive Neutrino Mass: Effects of Fluctuations of the Dark Matter Field

    Soham Sahasrabuddhe, Manibrata Sen, Alexei Y. Smirnov

    The paper simulates interference patterns of ultralight scalar dark matter with quartic self-interaction and uses them to compute fluctuation-induced distortions of JUNO and T2K oscillation probabilities in the refractive neutrino mass scenario. It finds the complex-field case viable for $m_\phi \lesssim 10^{-16}$ eV, with repulsive self-interactions reducing the distortion to $\Delta P\approx 0.07$ at larger masses, while real scalar fields remain constrained by Compton-frequency modulation over $10^{-22}\lesssim m_\phi\lesssim 10^{-10}$ eV.

    arXiv:2611.26549hep-phquality 9

    Neutrino mass from ultralight dark matter; phenomenology.
