    Spectra and Central Charges of Gauged Curvature Squared Supergravities in Five Dimensions

    Erol Aksoy, Cagatay Okyay, Mehmet Ozkan, Cemal Berfu Senisik

    The paper computes the exact spectra of off-shell gauged 5D N=2 supergravity with curvature-squared invariants around the supersymmetric AdS5 vacuum. It organizes the spectra into supermultiplets and checks ghost-freedom ranges. It also derives the central charges a, c and C_F, and analyzes the Gauss-Bonnet extension on AdS3×Σ2 backgrounds.

    arXiv:2611.15444hep-thgr-qcrelevance 8quality 8

    Curvature-squared 5D supergravity, ghosts, massive spin-2 multiplets, Weyl squared.

  2. 2

    Cosmological Signals from Finite-Lifetime Domain Wall Networks

    Fengwei Yang, Kylar Greene, Aurora Ireland, Yuhsin Tsai

    The paper uses the unconnected segment model to derive analytic scalar and tensor power spectra sourced by metastable domain wall networks. It finds a fixed tensor-to-scalar ratio during scaling and a spectrum peaked at the collapse scale. It then bounds the wall energy density at collapse from curvature perturbation constraints and shows that the resulting CMB B-mode signal can be comparable to an inflationary r~0.03.

    arXiv:2611.15860astro-ph.COhep-phrelevance 8quality 8

    Gravitational waves/B-modes from domain wall networks; tensor spectrum, topological defects.

  3. 3

    How to find regular black-hole islands in the swamp

    Astrid Eichhorn, Pedro G. S. Fernandes, Verena Koeder

    The authors assume regularity builds up gradually order by order in the gravitational EFT with a single new-physics scale, and translate this into monotonicity conditions on the deviations of the static black-hole metric functions from Schwarzschild. They apply five such conditions to constrain the order-eight EFT coefficients (with and without a light scalar), find an 'island' of allowed couplings, and check whether string theory and asymptotically safe gravity values fall inside it.

    arXiv:2611.15795hep-thgr-qcrelevance 6quality 7

    Higher-curvature EFT regular black holes, constraints on coefficients, asymptotic safety.

  4. 4

    Gauge aspects of teleparallel gravity and Noether's second theorem

    Sebastian Brezina, Martin Krššák

    The paper applies Noether's second theorem to TEGR and finds that its gauge symmetries are diffeomorphisms and local Lorentz transformations, with no independent translational gauge symmetry. It also argues that the Noether identities force Levi-Civita matter coupling, including for spinors, so that TEGR and GR remain equivalent with matter.

    arXiv:2611.17147gr-qcrelevance 7quality 7

    Teleparallel gravity gauge structure and Noether identities.

  5. 5

    D(-1)/D7 bound states in supergravity

    Gongrui Cheng, Klaas Parmentier, Thomas Van Riet

    The paper constructs supersymmetric codimension-two D(-1)/D7 bound-state solutions in Euclidean type IIB supergravity with complementary D3 fluxes, reducing the BPS equations to two holomorphic functions plus a constraint. It presents explicit solutions, including a constant-dilaton one that flows to a R×S^1×T^8 scaling geometry, and analyzes running deformations.

    arXiv:2611.15423hep-threlevance 6quality 8

    Codimension-two supergravity solutions in IIB with holomorphic functions and axio-dilaton; D7 branes.

  6. 6

    Radiative stability of species-separated Kaluza--Klein spectra in warped geometry

    M. A. Anacleto, F. A. Brito, E. Passos, Joao R. L. Santos, J. A. V. Campos, I. E. L. M. Santana

    The paper asks whether species-dependent fifth-direction speeds in a warped extra dimension survive radiative corrections. Quark loops keep the gluon speed between the bare speeds, but gluon exchange pulls the quark speed order-one toward the gluon speed. The light-gluon benchmark therefore has no perturbative protection, while the slow-quark benchmark is about ten times more robust.

    arXiv:2611.15753hep-phgr-qchep-threlevance 6quality 6

    Warped extra dimension KK spectra; follow-up to a wanted paper.

  7. 7

    Weak null singularities for the Einstein--Vlasov system

    Dawei Shen, Yuefeng Song, Jingbo Wan

    The paper proves local existence of Einstein–Vlasov solutions (massive and massless) from characteristic data with a weak null singularity. The metric extends continuously, the energy-momentum tensor stays bounded and continuous, and Christoffel symbols are not square integrable. Under extra curvature assumptions the spacetime is Lipschitz inextendible. An appendix shows that Einstein–scalar field data can instead produce instantaneous Ricci blow-up.

    arXiv:2611.15318gr-qcmath-phmath.APrelevance 4quality 8

    Weak null singularities in Einstein-Vlasov; singularity mathematics in GR.

  8. 8

    1-loop partition function of 11d supergravity on $\mathrm{AdS}_4\times S^7/\mathbb{Z}_k$: matching $A(k)$ in ABJM free energy

    Stefan A. Kurlyand, Arkady A. Tseytlin

    The paper shows that adding the harmonic 2-form sector of the 3-form field on AdS4 to the KK-tower one-loop supergravity result, with Z_k projection and zeta regularization, reproduces the exact ABJM free energy constant A(k). It extends the method to the ADHM theory with localized 7d SU(p) vector multiplet modes, again matching the localization result.

    arXiv:2611.15510hep-threlevance 4quality 8

    Supergravity one-loop on AdS4 x S7/Z_k; M-theory but not listed topics.

  9. 9

    From Matched Black-Hole Scalar Charges to Gravitational-Wave Inspirals in a Massive Scalar--Gauss--Bonnet Effective Theory

    Jie Wu, Mengfei Sun, Jin Li

    The paper matches near- and far-zone solutions for the curvature-sourced massive scalar field of an isolated Schwarzschild black hole in massive sGB gravity. It finds no linear scalar-mass correction to the static coefficient, with the first correction quadratic. It then uses the resulting charges in leading-PN binary dynamics, dipole and quadrupole scalar radiation thresholds, and inspiral phasing, and also constructs the associated breathing and longitudinal tidal responses.

    arXiv:2611.14934gr-qcrelevance 6quality 7

    Massive scalar-Gauss-Bonnet gravity black holes and inspirals.

  10. 10

    Dilution of cosmological relics and gravitational waves from a supercooled electroweak phase transition in classically scale-invariant models

    Arnab Chaudhuri

    The paper studies a strongly supercooled single-step electroweak transition in a classically scale-invariant singlet-plus-N scalars model with a Gildener-Weinberg dilaton. It computes bounce, percolation and completion, finds reheating near the vacuum-energy scale, and derives the resulting entropy dilution of relics (baryon asymmetry, thermal dark matter, earlier GWs), plus a millihertz GW signal in the LISA band, with systematic uncertainties quantified.

    arXiv:2611.16845hep-phrelevance 6quality 7

    Classically scale-invariant SM extension, Gildener-Weinberg; supercooled transition focus though.

  11. 11

    Critical schemes, jet syzygies, and twisted periods

    Monica Jinwoo Kang

    The paper shows that requiring a specific minimal invariant syzygy among the first jet equations fixes the Jacobian-ring scheme structure of certain potentials. These potentials are determinant-coefficient polynomials on sl_N and so_2N with critical locus {M^p=0}, plus a hypergeometric family on C^2/Z_n. It identifies the syzygy with the twisted period equation, finds an so(8) triality doublet, and proves that restriction to Slodowy slices commutes with forming critical schemes.

    arXiv:2611.14631math.AGhep-thmath.ACmath.RTcross-listrelevance 2quality 8

    Math of critical schemes tied to Argyres-Douglas indices.

    Exact model of a gravitational wave and electromagnetic radiation in DHOST gravity theory: wave solutions and testable effects

    Konstantin E. Osetrin

    Solves Maxwell's equations on an exact Petrov type N gravitational-wave background in null coordinates, finding that the EM wave must propagate co-directionally with the GW and acts as a pure-radiation source. It then inserts this into DHOST Ia field equations to argue for a modified EM frequency/phase (frequency drift) distinguishing DHOST from GR despite identical metrics.

    arXiv:2611.15414gr-qcastro-ph.HEhep-thmath-phrelevance 6quality 5

    Exact wave solutions in DHOST gravity with testable effects; modified gravity solutions.

    Quantum and classical thermal equilibrium for probabilistic classical fields

    C. Wetterich

    The paper shows that a classical probability distribution for free fields can reproduce the Planck spectrum (quantum thermal equilibrium) as an alternative to the Rayleigh-Jeans equilibrium. It constructs a classical-quantum map between classical distributions and quantum density matrices, and uses 1+1D classical lattice simulations with interactions to compare which equilibrium is approached dynamically.

    arXiv:2611.15749hep-thhep-latquant-phrelevance 2quality 7

    Classical probabilistic fields and quantum formalism; marginal.

    New Physics in Ultra-High-Energy Neutrino Flavor

    Samiur R. Mir, Carlos A. Argüelles, Vedran Brdar

    The paper projects the sensitivity of future UHE neutrino flavor measurements (in-ice radio plus Earth-skimming tau channels) to isotropic Lorentz invariance violation operators of dimension 3-8. It finds improvements over current bounds of up to about 20 orders of magnitude for d=8, and up to an order of magnitude better sensitivity from three-flavor information than from Earth-skimming taus alone.

    ESSnuSB Collaboration, J. Aguilar, D. Barčot, E. Baussan, A. K. Bhattacharyya, M. Blennow, M. Bogomilov, B. Bolling, E. Bouquerel, F. Bramati, A. Branca, G. Brunetti + 89 more

    A Dark-Sector Mass Flip-Flop Mechanism for the ANITA-IV Events

    Dibya S. Chattopadhyay, Carlos A. Argüelles, Vedran Brdar

    The paper proposes that an ultralight scalar sourced by Earth matter inverts the mass ordering of two dark fermions, so that an incoming UHE N1 converts to N2 underground and N2 decays in the atmosphere to initiate an upgoing air shower. The decays are kinematically blocked at IceCube depths. Two benchmark points are fitted to the four ANITA-IV events (angular fit p=0.098 and 0.023), and about three events are predicted for PUEO's first flight.

    arXiv:2611.17328hep-phastro-ph.HEhep-exrelevance 0quality 7

    Neutrino phenomenology, out of scope.
