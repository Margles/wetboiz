    Hidden wormholes from an action principle in $D=4$

    Francesco Del Porro, Alessia Platania, Samuele Silveravalle

    The paper finds numerical static spherically symmetric solutions of GR plus the cubic Weyl (Goroff-Sagnotti) operator. These are black holes with an interior wormhole throat leading to a Kantowski-Sachs cosmological region, with no inner horizon and no curvature singularity. Dynamical stability and formation are left open.

    arXiv:2611.21712gr-qchep-threlevance 6quality 8

    Cubic-curvature gravity EFT with only graviton; regular black holes and wormholes.

  2. 2

    Regular/Singular Black Hole Thermodynamics from Renormalised Volume/Area

    Gabriel Arenas-Henriquez, Rodrigo Olea, David Rivera-Betancour

    The paper studies the finite mismatch between holographically and topologically renormalised Euclidean actions of the 4D slowly accelerating AdS C-metric. It shows the mismatch equals tension-weighted Euler terms of the renormalised string worldsheet areas, matching the Gauss-Bonnet entropy at the conical points, and gives a first law in which the difference of renormalised areas sets the relative tension work.

    arXiv:2611.22107hep-thgr-qcrelevance 6quality 8

    Conformal gravity renormalised volume, AdS C-metric with conical strings, Gauss-Bonnet entropy.

  3. 3

    5d $\mathbb{Z}_{n}$ S-folds, quotient curves, and isogenies

    Sung-Soo Kim, Song Liu, Mudassar Sabir, Futoshi Yagi

    The paper constructs Seiberg-Witten curves for Z_3, Z_4 and Z_6 S-folds of 5d rank-1 SCFTs. It does so both by a coordinate quotient of the 5-brane web curve and by tuning E_6, E_7 and E_8 curve masses, and finds that the two constructions agree in Weierstrass form. It also reproduces the curves as cyclic n-isogenies via Vélu's formulas, and it discusses a Z_5 quotient curve.

    arXiv:2611.22811hep-threlevance 6quality 8

    5d S-folds, Seiberg-Witten curves, 7-branes; similar to unwanted sibling paper.

    Cosmic Censorship in the Landscape of SUSY-Broken AdS Vacua

    Netta Engelhardt, Richard Nally

    The paper proves that an EFT of scalars coupled to gravity in AdS satisfies the Penrose Inequality and a positive mass theorem if a 'weak fake superpotential' exists. It shows this holds globally for skew-whiffed DGKT vacua and for some, but not all, of 66 SUSY-broken KKLT AdS vacua. In the cases studied, PI violations track false vacuum decay.

    arXiv:2611.21722hep-thgr-qcrelevance 4quality 8

    Non-SUSY AdS vacua stability in string theory; cosmic censorship, swampland-adjacent.

    The SymTFT and Quantum Phases of Matter

    Salvatore D. Pace, Sakura Schafer-Nameki, Carolyn Zhang

    An invited Annual Review introducing the SymTFT sandwich construction, using abelian group symmetries and then fusion category symmetries in 1+1d to classify gapped phases, anomalies, and gapless-phase structure. It also surveys extensions to higher dimensions, continuous, fermionic, mixed-state, and spacetime symmetries, with open problems.

    arXiv:2611.21715cond-mat.str-elhep-phhep-thquant-phcross-listrelevance 4quality 8

    SymTFT review on generalized symmetries, mostly condensed matter oriented.

    From Top to Bottom: Quark Mass Hierarchies in Heterotic String Theory

    Yuri Chervonyi, Fabian Ruehle, Nenad Tomasev

    The paper proposes two complex-structure mechanisms for tree-level Yukawa hierarchies in heterotic line bundle models and shows both are obstructed on the tetra-quadric CICY 7862. It then scans the CICY databases for a model with a rank-three up-quark Yukawa matrix, finding CICY 5273 Model 500, where numerical Calabi-Yau metrics, HYM connections and harmonic forms combined with RG running give realistic t, c, u, b mass hierarchies.

    arXiv:2611.21751hep-thcs.AIrelevance 3quality 7

    Heterotic string phenomenology of quark masses; not swampland focus.

    Giant gravitons without giant gravitons

    Blake J. Bottesi, James T. Liu

    The authors apply Murthy's matrix-integral expansion to a free bosonic d-matrix U(N) model and rearrange it into a giant-graviton-like expansion with d types of giants and a quiver of bifundamentals. Some giant indices depend on N through higher-order poles, which they trace to degenerate poles in the integrand, and they discuss whether this hints at a brane interpretation.

    arXiv:2611.21754hep-threlevance 3quality 7

    Giant graviton expansion in matrix model; holography adjacent, weak match.

  8. 8

    Beyond the point lens: radial structure and the horizon in wave-optics gravitational lensing

    Alice Garoffolo, Gianmassimo Tasinato

    The paper builds a Born series around the exact Newtonian point-lens wave, which resums the long-range phase, to compute scalar-wave lensing corrections from inverse-power radial channels. It shows that the direct wave responds to the inverse-square term, and that for Schwarzschild the horizon enters as an absorptive contact term matching the horizon-area cross section and largely cancelling the finite-distance optical correction for an aligned source.

    arXiv:2611.22618gr-qcastro-ph.COhep-threlevance 2quality 8

    Wave-optics gravitational lensing; general gravity but no listed topic.

    StringQFT: A Multiuse Textbook Resource for AI-Assisted Theoretical Physics

    Xingyang Yu

    The paper introduces StringQFT, an 18-volume AI-assisted textbook collection on string constructions of QFT, with a benchmark inventory of 1,073 items, a retrieval corpus, 21 private training examples, and 19 calculation tools for agents. It illustrates the pipeline with a Gopakumar–Vafa coefficient inversion and roundtrip check, without reporting any model evaluations.

    arXiv:2611.23225hep-thcs.AIrelevance 4quality 5

    Textbook resource covering brane webs, geometric engineering, F-theory; AI infrastructure focus.

    A Universal Axion in a Twin-QCD Sector

    Stuart Raby, Scott Watson

    The paper studies a single axion coupled to both visible and twin QCD, where the twin sector's larger confinement scale (5x) enhances the axion mass by about 25 if the strong-CP minima align. It then uses lattice susceptibilities and a two-temperature cosmology to estimate axion and twin-baryon dark-matter abundances in a conditional benchmark, noting the needed UV and cosmological conditions.

    arXiv:2611.23015hep-phhep-threlevance 1quality 6

    Axion twin-QCD dark matter; out of scope.
