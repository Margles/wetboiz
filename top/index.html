  1. 1

    What Comes Out Of Monopole-Fermion Scattering?

    David Tong

    Studies massless chiral fermions scattering off a monopole in the s-wave using bosonisation and shows the outgoing state carries a soft cloud of infinitely many particle-antiparticle pairs, with $n(\omega)\sim1/\omega$. It computes energy and charge correlators in 3+1d, finding logarithmically growing charge fluctuations, and discusses mass deformations.

  2. 2

    Geometric Quantization of the Giant Graviton Master Moduli Space

    Kasia Budzik, Davide Gaiotto

    The paper reorganizes finite-$N$ $U(N)$ gauge-invariant BPS operators as single-trace plus determinant operators modulo "open loop equations", interpreted as a gauged geometric quantization of the Giant Graviton moduli space. It builds BRST/BV complexes conjectured to categorify Murthy's Giant Graviton expansion, and verifies this with explicit cohomology computations in examples.

    Giant graviton moduli with BRST complexes; only loosely related.

  3. 3

    A Note On Lattice Definition of Gauge Theories With Chiral Fermions

    Edward Witten

    The paper gives a variant of Craig's lattice regularization of chiral gauge theories, interpolating the lattice gauge field to the continuum and coupling it to continuum chiral fermions via the $\eta$-invariant. It argues this works for any gauge group and anomaly-free representation and is computable in principle but not by Monte Carlo.
